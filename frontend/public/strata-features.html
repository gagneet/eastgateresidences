Trust Accounting — Payment Intelligence

The 80% Problem, Solved

Auto-Match Most Owner Payments. Investigate the Rest with Evidence. The single biggest time sink in strata accounting is identifying which incoming bank transfer belongs to which lot. StrataOS handles this through an eight-layer matching engine that auto-allocates the majority of payments on day one and surfaces the rest for review with full evidence — not as a mystery to solve but as a decision to confirm.

🎯

Eight-Layer Matching Engine

Every incoming bank transaction passes through eight layers of identification logic. Exact CRN or DRN matches with verified checksums auto-allocate immediately. Partial matches, fuzzy name matches, amount-and-timing patterns, and remittance advice parsing each contribute when the obvious answer isn't available.

WHAT THIS REPLACES
✓ Manual cross-referencing of bank descriptions against owner names
✓ Spreadsheet tracking of unidentified payments
✓ Email chains asking owners to confirm what they paid
✓ Quarterly write-offs of payments no one could trace
🔗

Cryptographic Audit Chain

Every financial transaction is recorded as an immutable event in an append-only ledger. At end of close, the ledger can be hashed into a Merkle seal that chains forward to the next seal. Retroactive tampering — by any user, including system administrators — is detectable when the seal is verified.

WHAT YOUR AUDITOR GETS
✓ Complete transaction history with user attribution on every line
✓ Merkle seal evidence for closed ledger periods
✓ Period-locked closes that cannot be reopened by accident
✓ Three-way reconciliation enforced at month-end before close is permitted
🔄

Mid-Quarter Ownership Transfers Handled Correctly

When a unit sells halfway through a quarter, the platform tracks both who owned the lot when the levy was raised and who pays each instalment after settlement. Pro-rata calculations for solicitors at settlement are produced automatically.

WHAT THIS PREVENTS
✓ Misallocated payments after a sale
✓ Owner statements mailed to the previous owner
✓ Manual settlement adjustment calculations
✓ Disputes about which owner is liable for which arrears
🔌

Provider-Agnostic Payment Architecture

The platform doesn't require any specific bank, biller, or payment provider relationship. Small agencies operate on monthly CSV bank statement uploads and supervised matching. Provider adapters can be added through the same integration boundary as external bank-feed relationships mature, without rewriting the trust ledger.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU
✓ Onboard immediately without commercial provider relationships
✓ Keep bank-feed approval policy controlled per building
✓ No vendor lock-in — switch providers without changing your workflow
✓ Future payment rails (PayTo, Peppol e-invoicing) supported as adapters
The architecture was built for change. Australian payment rails are in active flux — AusPayNet removed the June 2030 BECS decommission target (Dec 2025); a new A2A Payments Roadmap is expected in 2026. PayTo is progressively replacing direct debit (18M overlay agreements by end-2025), Peppol e-invoicing becomes default in federal procurement from July 2026, and Open Banking access is maturing under the Consumer Data Right (non-bank lender obligations from July 2026). StrataOS was designed specifically to absorb these changes as adapter swaps rather than platform rewrites. Your trust ledger doesn't care which providers your buildings use.

Switching Made Simple

Switching from StrataMax or MRI?
Here's Exactly How It Works.

Data migration is the #1 reason Strata Managers stay on legacy software. We've designed a 3-phase process so you know exactly what to expect before you commit.

1

Export Your Data

Export your building data from your current system. We accept standard formats from all major platforms.

📄 StrataMax: Levy ledger CSV, owner roll, financial statements
📄 MRI Strata Master: Unit roll, levy schedule, transaction history
📄 Any system: Excel/CSV with unit and owner data
⏱ Time from you: 1–2 hours to locate and export files
2

We Import & Validate

Our team maps your data into StrataOS, validates balances against your last reconciliation, and runs a parallel test so you can verify everything before going live.

✅ Unit roll, lot entitlements, unit interests
✅ Owner names, emails, contact details
✅ Opening levy balances per lot
✅ Trust account opening balance
✅ Historical levy transactions (where available)
⏱ Our turnaround: 3–5 business days for standard migration
3

Go Live on Your Terms

You choose the go-live date. We run a live training session with your team, send owner welcome emails, and stay on-call for the first 30 days.

✅ Staff training session (live video call)
✅ Owner portal welcome emails dispatched
✅ 30-day hypercare support period
✅ Your old system stays active throughout
⏱ Typical total timeline: 2–4 weeks from decision to go-live

Common Questions About Switching

Will I lose my historical levy data?
No. We import all available historical transaction data. Where export capability is limited, we import opening balances as of migration date.
Can I run both systems during transition?
Yes — and we recommend it. Your existing system stays active throughout migration and the 30-day hypercare period. Cancel it only once you're confident.
What about our trust account reconciliation history?
Your audited accounts remain in your current system as the historical record. StrataOS takes over from migration date with a validated opening balance — your auditor can access both.
What does migration cost?
Included at no extra cost for buildings up to 150 lots. Larger or complex portfolio migrations are quoted individually — typically $1,000–$3,000 for custom data mapping.
