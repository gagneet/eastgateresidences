How the Platform Creates Value — by Persona

🏢 For the Strata Manager

  • Replace spreadsheets with a live levy ledger — know who owes what, right now
  • Trust reconciliation runs automatically — bank balance, portal balance and internal ledger always agree (or flag exceptions)
  • Invoice OCR eliminates manual data entry — AI extracts vendor, amount, ABN and GST line items
  • 79-item PPM schedule auto-generates work orders and notifies contractors — nothing falls through the cracks
  • Regulatory compliance (ACT/NSW) rules are encoded as data — stay ahead of statutory changes without code releases
  • One dashboard shows all three buildings — portfolio-level KPIs in a single view
  • 130+ feature toggles let you roll out or restrict features per building without any code deployment

👥 For the Executive Committee

  • See the building's financial health score (0–100) and reserve adequacy at a glance — no waiting for the manager's report
  • Capital shock modelling shows what happens to levies if a major asset fails unexpectedly
  • Levy fairness index reveals whether apartments subsidise townhouses (or vice versa) — data for levy reform decisions
  • Investment grade (A–F) gives an objective picture of how the building compares to the market
  • Conflict of interest register makes governance transparent — declarations are logged against specific motions
  • What-if levy scenarios let the committee test different levy increase options before the AGM
  • All approvals (invoices, quotes, special levies) are tracked with a full decision audit trail

🏠 For Owners & Residents

  • See exactly what is owed, when it is due and download receipts — no phone calls to the manager needed
  • True cost of ownership shows levies + council rates + water + insurance in one 10-year view
  • Property health score updated weekly — know the building's condition without reading reports
  • Submit a maintenance request in plain English — the NLP engine routes it to the right contractor automatically
  • Vote on community proposals and AGM motions from a phone — no need to be physically present
  • Volunteer credits program rewards community contribution with levy offsets
  • Community marketplace, chat and directory build genuine neighbourly engagement
