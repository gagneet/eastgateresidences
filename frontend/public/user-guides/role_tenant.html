Tenant Resident Handbook

Welcome to your building! As a tenant, you have access to a variety of community and maintenance tools to make your stay comfortable and engaging.

Key Responsibilities
  • Keep your contact details updated in My Profile.
  • Report maintenance issues promptly via the Maintenance Tracker.
  • Respect community guidelines when using Facility Bookings.

Registration & Building Selection

To create your tenant account, visit /register and select your building from the cards displayed. Choose Tenant as your role and enter your unit number. Your account will be reviewed by the Strata Manager before activation.

  • The platform automatically sets your building context after login — no manual step needed if you belong to one building.
  • Contact your property manager or strata manager if you do not see your building listed.

Communication

  • Notice Board: Stay informed about building-wide news, maintenance schedules, or community updates.
  • Community Chat: Meet your neighbors and participate in local discussions.
  • Private Messages: Send direct messages to other residents.

Community

  • Resident Directory: Find and connect with other residents who have opted into the directory.
  • Document Library: Access public documents like building rules, moving procedures, and emergency contact lists.
  • Facility Bookings: Book shared spaces like the BBQ area or book the lift for your move-in/move-out.
  • Marketplace: Browse local listings or sell items you no longer need.

Maintenance Requests — Tenant Portal

Navigate to Dashboard → My Tenancy → Maintenance Requests. The Tenant Maintenance Portal lets you submit, track, and communicate about repair requests directly with your owner, real estate agent, or building manager.

Submitting a Request

Click Submit Request and fill in:

  • Issue Title — brief summary (e.g. "Leaking tap in bathroom")
  • Description — full details of the problem
  • Urgency — Routine / Urgent / Emergency
  • Photos — attach photos for faster diagnosis
  • Preferred Access Times — when you're available for a tradesperson
Emergency requests (no hot water, gas leak, electrical fault, security breach) are escalated immediately. Also call 000 if there is an immediate safety risk.

Tracking Your Request

Each request progresses through these stages:

  1. Submitted — your request is received
  2. Acknowledged — the owner/agent/manager has reviewed it
  3. Assigned — a tradesperson has been assigned
  4. In Progress — works are underway
  5. Completed — repair is finished

You will receive a notification at each stage change. You can also send messages back and forth with the responsible party in the request thread.

Community OS — New Features (April 2026)

The following features were added as part of the Community OS update. All are enabled by default and accessible from your dashboard.

Smart Request — Submit & Track Queries

Navigate to Dashboard → Smart Request or Dashboard → Requests.

Use the 3-step Smart Request tool to submit any query in plain English. The system automatically classifies it and sets an SLA response window.

  1. Step 1 — Category: Choose from Maintenance Issue, Levy Query, By-Law Query, Document Request, Noise Complaint, or Other.
  2. Step 2 — Details: Enter a subject and description. Optionally attach photos or documents.
  3. Step 3 — Done: Your confirmation screen shows the expected SLA response window.

Switch to the My Requests tab to track all your submitted requests. Click any request to view its full status timeline (Received → Classified → Assigned → Resolved).

SLA Commitment: Noise complaints — triaged within 4 hours. Maintenance issues — 1 business day. All other requests — 2 business days.

Tenancy Passport — Verified Residency Credential

Navigate to Dashboard → My Passport.

Your Tenancy Passport is a portable, verified residency credential you can share with real estate agents, banks, or prospective landlords to prove your rental history and responsible tenancy.

  • Residency Score (0–100): An overall grade (A–D) summarising your tenancy record at this building.
  • Score components:
    • Lease Compliance — on-time rent, by-law adherence
    • Maintenance Response — how promptly you respond to maintenance notices
    • Community Engagement — participation in building events and activities
    • Noise/Disturbance Record — any recorded complaints or incidents
  • Download PDF: Generate a signed, dated PDF credential to present to agents or banks.
  • Public verify link: Share a mobile-optimised verification URL — recipients can confirm your score without a platform login.
Tip: A strong Tenancy Passport score supports rental applications and lease renewal negotiations. Improve your score by responding promptly to maintenance notices and engaging in community events.

Volunteer Credits — Earn Recognition

Navigate to Dashboard → Financials → My Finances.

Volunteer Credits are earned by participating in building events, working bees, or committee activities. Credits are recorded in your My Finances page and contribute positively to your Tenancy Passport score.

Morning Card — Daily Personalised Summary

On your Dashboard Home, a Morning Card now appears each time you log in. As a tenant it highlights the single most relevant item:

  • Upcoming lease renewal date reminder
  • Status of an open noise complaint or request
  • Notification of an upcoming building inspection

Click the card's button to navigate directly to the relevant section.

Weekly Notification Digest

Every Sunday at 6:00 PM AEST you will receive a Weekly Notification Digest email summarising your past week's unread notifications with direct links to each item.

To opt out, navigate to Dashboard → Email Preferences and toggle off Digest Emails.
