Invoices & Quotes

Upload contractor invoices and quotes — your building automatically reads the key details and posts the expense to the financial ledger

What does this feature do?

When your building receives a PDF invoice or quote from a contractor, you no longer need to manually re-type the vendor name, amounts, GST and dates. Simply upload the document and the system uses AI document reading to extract the key fields automatically.

You review the extracted information, make any corrections, choose the fund (Administrative or Sinking), then click Confirm. The system immediately:

  • Records an expense transaction in your financial ledger
  • Generates a payment receipt PDF you can download and file
  • Keeps the original document linked to the record
Supported formats: PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP — up to 20 MB per file.

Uploading an invoice or quote

1
Go to Dashboard → Invoices & Quotes.
2
Click Upload Invoice or Upload Quote in the top-right corner. A dialog will open.
3
Drag and drop your PDF or image file onto the upload area, or click the area to open a file picker.
4
Wait a moment while the system reads the document. You will see a "Parsing with Claude Vision…" message. This usually takes 5–15 seconds.
5
The Review screen appears with the extracted fields pre-filled. Check the details carefully before confirming.

Reviewing the extracted details

After upload, you will see a review form with the fields the system read from your document. An OCR badge in the top-right of the form shows which engine was used and its confidence level:

  • Claude Vision — 94% confidence — extracted using AI document analysis (primary)
  • Mindee — 82% confidence — extracted using a specialist invoice parser (fallback)
  • Manual entry — fields are blank; please fill in manually

Review and correct the following fields:

  • Vendor / Supplier Name — required
  • ABN — optional but recommended for GST records
  • Invoice Number — the reference from the vendor's document
  • Invoice Date — required
  • Due Date — optional
  • Fund — choose Administrative Fund (day-to-day expenses) or Sinking Fund (capital works and major replacements)
  • Category — e.g. Plumbing, Electrical, General Maintenance
  • Line Items — individual work items; rows can be added, edited, or removed
  • Subtotal, GST, Total — auto-calculated when you edit line items; or enter directly if there are no line items
Important: Always verify the total amount and vendor name before confirming. Once confirmed, the expense is written to the financial ledger and the record is retained permanently (7-year statutory requirement).

Confirming and posting to the ledger

1
After reviewing all fields, click Confirm & Post to Ledger.
2
The system creates an expense transaction in the financial ledger under the fund and category you selected, and generates a payment receipt PDF.
3
The confirmation screen appears. Click Download Receipt PDF to save the receipt for your records.
4
Click Done to close the dialog. The invoice now appears in the list with a Confirmed status.
Where does the expense appear? The transaction is visible in your building's Finance page under the Expenses tab, in the fund and category you selected. It will also appear in budget-vs-actual reports.

The invoice list

The Invoices & Quotes page shows all documents uploaded for your building, with summary stats at the top:

  • Total Uploaded — all invoices and quotes, any status
  • Pending Review — uploaded but not yet confirmed (highlighted in amber if any exist)
  • Confirmed — posted to the ledger
  • Total Confirmed — sum of all confirmed amounts

Status badges

  • Pending Review — uploaded and parsed, waiting for a manager to review and confirm
  • Confirmed — expense posted to ledger; record is permanent

Searching and filtering

Use the search bar to filter by vendor name, invoice number, or filename. Use the status filter dropdown to show only Pending Review or only Confirmed records.

Downloading a receipt

Receipts are available for all Confirmed invoices. There are two ways to download:

  • From the confirmation screen immediately after confirming, click Download Receipt PDF.
  • From the invoice list, click the download icon (⬇) in the Actions column of any confirmed invoice.

Deleting a pending invoice

Only Pending Review invoices can be deleted — for example if you uploaded the wrong document. Confirmed invoices are retained permanently to satisfy the 7-year strata record-keeping requirement.

To delete: click the trash icon in the Actions column and confirm the prompt.

Confirmed invoices cannot be deleted. If an invoice was confirmed in error, contact your strata manager to issue a correcting entry in the financial ledger.

Frequently asked questions

The OCR missed some fields or got the amount wrong. What should I do?
Simply correct the fields in the review form before confirming. The system always gives you the chance to check and fix extracted data — nothing is posted to the ledger until you click Confirm.
The upload is stuck on "Parsing with Claude Vision…"
This can happen with scanned images that are very low resolution or heavily rotated. Wait up to 30 seconds. If the upload fails, try again with a higher-quality scan. If the fields come back blank, fill them in manually and confirm.
Can I upload a quote that I haven't paid yet?
Yes. Upload it as a Quote and leave it in Pending Review status. It will not be posted to the ledger until you confirm it. When you are ready to record the payment, open the record (or re-upload the final invoice) and confirm at that point.
Which fund should I choose?
Administrative Fund — routine operating expenses: maintenance, repairs, cleaning, insurance, management fees, pest control, landscaping.
Sinking Fund — capital works and major replacements: roof replacement, lift upgrades, structural repairs, car park resurfacing.
If in doubt, ask your strata manager.
Is the original PDF stored?
Yes. The original file is stored securely with the record and is available to authorised building managers.
What happens if I close the dialog before confirming?
The document has already been uploaded and a Pending Review record exists. You can find it in the invoice list and confirm it later. It will not be posted to the ledger until confirmed.
