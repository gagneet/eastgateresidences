Agency Portfolio Analytics Guide

This guide is for Strata Admins who manage a portfolio of buildings. The Agency Analytics dashboard gives you a cross-building view of your entire portfolio's performance — without having to open each building one by one.

Accessing Agency Analytics

  1. Log in as a strata admin.
  2. From the main navigation, go to Dashboard → Agency Analytics.
  3. The dashboard loads showing aggregate data across all buildings assigned to your company.
Tip: You can filter the agency analytics by management entity (team or office) if your company is structured into multiple teams. Use the Entity filter at the top of the page to narrow down to a specific group of buildings.

Viewing Cross-Building KPIs

The top section of the Agency Analytics page shows portfolio-wide summary cards:

  • Total buildings managed — number of active buildings in your portfolio.
  • Overall levy collection rate — the weighted average collection rate across all buildings. Aim for above 90%.
  • Total arrears — the combined arrears balance owed across all buildings. This is the number your principal is most likely to ask about.
  • Compliance overdue (portfolio) — total count of overdue compliance items across all buildings. Even one building with a critical overdue item will show here.
  • SLA breach count — total number of maintenance requests in breach across the portfolio, broken down by building.
  • Buildings needing attention — a quick count of buildings currently rated red or amber, with links to each.

Identifying Underperforming Buildings

The Building Performance Table lists every building in your portfolio with a row-level RAG status. Columns include:

  • Collection rate (this quarter)
  • Arrears as a percentage of annual levy
  • Compliance items overdue
  • Open maintenance requests
  • SLA breach count
  • Budget vs. actual variance

Sort by any column to identify your worst performers. Buildings coloured red in three or more columns may need a management review or additional resource allocation.

Important: A building that appears red on levy collection but green on compliance and maintenance is a different risk profile from one that is red across the board. Consider each building's combination of signals before escalating.

Arrears Hotspots Across the Portfolio

Click Arrears Deep Dive on the Agency Analytics page to see:

  • Which buildings have the highest total arrears in dollar terms.
  • Which buildings have arrears that are increasing month on month.
  • The average days overdue for unpaid levies, by building.
  • A breakdown of arrears by age bucket (0–30 days, 31–60 days, 60+ days) across the portfolio.

Use this view to decide where to apply additional follow-up resources or escalate to a debt collection referral conversation with the EC.

Compliance Overdue Across the Portfolio

The Compliance Overview panel shows a grid of compliance categories (fire safety, lifts, pool, electrical, etc.) across all buildings. Red cells indicate an item that is currently overdue for that building. You can:

  • Click any red cell to open that building's compliance detail directly.
  • Export the grid as a spreadsheet for reporting to your principal.
  • Set up automatic email alerts for any compliance item that becomes overdue (available in Agency Settings).

Budget vs. Actual for Each Building

The Financial Variance panel shows each building's budget adherence for the current financial year. Buildings that are significantly over budget in any category are highlighted in amber or red.

  • Over-budget categories are listed with the variance amount and the responsible manager.
  • This view helps you identify where buildings may need a budget revision or where unexpected costs have occurred.

Drilling Down into a Specific Building

From any panel on the Agency Analytics page, you can click a building name or any highlighted figure to go directly into that building's detailed view. The portal switches your building context automatically — you do not need to use the building switcher manually.

To return to the agency-level view, use the Back to Portfolio link at the top of the screen.
