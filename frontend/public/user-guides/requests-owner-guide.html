Requests & Applications — Owner Guide

Open Dashboard → Requests to search available requests, filter by category, start permitted forms and track progress from My Requests.

Available Owner Actions

  • Start pet, access-device, alteration, insurance claim, insurance enquiry and reimbursement requests when enabled for your building.
  • Use the dedicated Maintenance page for maintenance submissions.
  • Attach receipts for reimbursements and attach evidence/plans where the form asks for them.

Lot, Privacy And Restrictions

Lot-bound requests require your owner lot relationship in the active building. Staff can see what they need to review the request; other residents cannot see your private form details. Emergency threats are excluded: call emergency services first, then notify the building team.
