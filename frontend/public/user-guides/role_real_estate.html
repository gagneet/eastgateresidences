Real Estate Agent Handbook

Welcome to the your building platform as a Real Estate Agent. This guide explains how to use the platform to manage properties on behalf of owners, communicate with tenants, and oversee maintenance and inspections.

Your Role: As a Real Estate Agent, you are appointed by specific property owners to manage their units on their behalf. You will only see the properties explicitly assigned to you. Owners retain full visibility of their own properties and can interact with the system directly.

Role Overview

The Real Estate Agent role bridges the gap between property owners and tenants. Your responsibilities on this platform include:

  • Managing tenancies and lease records for assigned properties
  • Handling tenant maintenance requests (triage, assignment, follow-up)
  • Scheduling and documenting property inspections
  • Communicating with both owners and tenants via the messaging system
  • Monitoring lease renewal dates and alerting owners ahead of expiry
  • Recording rent transactions in the ledger

Agent Dashboard

Navigate to Dashboard → OwnerHub → Agent Dashboard. Your dashboard shows:

  • Properties Managed — total count of your assigned properties
  • Active Tenancies — current occupied properties
  • Pending Maintenance — open requests requiring action
  • Leases Expiring Soon — properties within 90 days of lease end

Managing Properties

Go to Dashboard → OwnerHub → My Properties. You will only see properties where the owner has listed your user ID in their assigned agents. For each property you can:

  • View property details (address, type, bedrooms, current rent)
  • See the active tenant and lease information
  • Check the property health score
  • Update property notes
Note: You cannot add, delete, or transfer ownership of properties. Only the property owner or a Super Admin can perform those actions.

Tenant Communications

All tenant communications flow through the platform's messaging system. When a tenant submits a maintenance request or sends a message, you receive a notification and can respond from the request thread.

To contact a tenant directly:

  1. Navigate to Dashboard → OwnerHub → Tenancies & Leases
  2. Select the relevant tenancy
  3. Use the Contact Tenant button to open a message thread

Maintenance Requests

Navigate to Dashboard → My Tenancy → Maintenance Requests or Dashboard → Agent Dashboard → Maintenance Queue. You will see all open requests for your assigned properties, sorted by urgency (Emergency first).

Processing a Request

  1. Click on the request to open the detail view.
  2. Review the tenant's description and photos.
  3. Click Update Request to:
    • Acknowledge the request (status → Acknowledged)
    • Assign a tradesperson (name, phone number)
    • Schedule a date for the work
    • Add a cost estimate
  4. Once works are complete, mark the request as Completed and record the actual cost.
  5. Close the request once confirmed with the tenant.

Urgency Levels

Level Description Target Response
🔴 Emergency Safety/habitability risk (gas, electrical, no hot water) Same day
🟡 Urgent Significant inconvenience (appliance failure, water leak) Within 3 days
🟢 Routine Minor issues, cosmetic repairs Within 2 weeks

Property Inspections

Navigate to Dashboard → OwnerHub → Inspections. You can schedule three types of inspections:

  • Entry (Ingoing) — Document property condition before a new tenant moves in.
  • Routine — Typically every 3–4 months during the tenancy.
  • Exit (Outgoing) — Assess condition after tenant vacates; compare with entry report.

For each inspection, record a condition score (1–10), notes, and attach the inspection report or photos. ACT law requires landlords to give tenants at least 7 days' notice for routine inspections.

Frequency Reminder: Under ACT residential tenancy law, routine inspections cannot occur more than 4 times per year.

Lease Renewals

The platform automatically flags leases expiring within 90 days in your Agent Dashboard. When a lease is approaching expiry:

  1. Contact the tenant to confirm their intentions (renew / vacate).
  2. If renewing, update the lease end date and new rent amount in Tenancies & Leases.
  3. Notify the owner of the renewal terms for approval.
  4. If tenant is vacating, schedule an exit inspection.

Access Permissions

As a Real Estate Agent, your access is deliberately scoped to protect owner privacy:

Feature Access
Properties (assigned only) ✅ View & Update notes
Tenancies (assigned properties) ✅ Full CRUD
Maintenance Requests ✅ View, Update, Assign
Inspections ✅ Create, View, Update
Building Finances (strata) ❌ Not accessible
Strata Levy Data ❌ Not accessible
Other owners' data ❌ Not accessible
Documents (building docs) ✅ View (public docs only)
Messaging ✅ Full access
