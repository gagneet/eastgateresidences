Strata Admin Portfolio Management Guide

This guide is for Strata Admin users — typically the principal or senior administrator of a strata management company. It covers how to set up and manage your company's portfolio of buildings on StrataOS.

Your role in brief: You create and manage the overall structure — the company, its teams (management entities), and which buildings belong to which team. Strata managers work within the structure you set up.

Setting Up Your Strata Management Company

When your company is first registered on StrataOS, a super admin will create your company profile. Once that is in place, you can manage it from Admin → Company Settings.

Here you can:

  • Update your company name, ABN, contact details, and logo.
  • Set your default levy notice templates and communication preferences.
  • View a summary of all buildings managed under your company.

Adding Buildings (Schemes) to Your Company

Each building you manage is called a scheme in StrataOS. To add a building:

  1. Go to Admin → Buildings and select Add New Building.
  2. Enter the building's unit plan number, address, and lot count.
  3. Assign it to a management entity (see below).
  4. StrataOS will create the building profile and prompt you to complete onboarding (levy setup, owner details, EC members).
Important: Buildings cannot be permanently deleted once created — they can only be archived. This is required under strata legislation for record retention. Use Archive Building if a building leaves your portfolio.

Creating and Managing Management Entities

A management entity is a team or office within your company. You might have one entity per state, one per region, or one per team leader. To create one:

  1. Go to Admin → Management Entities and select New Entity.
  2. Give it a name (for example, "Canberra Office" or "ACT Residential Team").
  3. Assign the buildings that belong to this entity.
  4. Optionally, set a team lead or default contact for the entity.

You can move a building from one entity to another at any time without losing data.

Assigning Strata Managers to Buildings

Once a building is assigned to an entity, you can appoint an individual strata manager to it:

  1. Open the building from Admin → Buildings.
  2. Go to the Management tab.
  3. Select Appoint Manager and choose from your company's strata managers.
  4. Set the contract start date and any relevant details.

The appointed manager will immediately see the building in their portfolio and can begin work. EC members and owners in that building will see the manager's name and contact details update automatically.

Tip: If a manager is on leave, you can temporarily appoint a relief manager to the building. The relief appears to owners and EC members just like the regular manager.

Viewing Agency-Level Analytics

As a strata admin, you have access to the Agency BI Analytics dashboard. This gives you a cross-building view of your entire portfolio:

  • Collection rate — which buildings have strong levy collection and which are falling behind.
  • Arrears hotspots — buildings with the highest outstanding amounts.
  • Compliance status — how many buildings across your portfolio have overdue compliance items.
  • Maintenance SLA performance — how quickly work orders are being resolved across the portfolio.
  • Budget vs. actuals — at a glance across all buildings.

Access this from Dashboard → Agency Analytics. You can drill down from any portfolio-level figure into the specific building that is driving it.
