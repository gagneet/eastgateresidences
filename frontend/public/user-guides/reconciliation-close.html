Closing a Reconciliation Period

This guide explains how to close a financial reconciliation period for your building. When the bank statement and journal entries match, the period closes automatically. If there is a discrepancy, a reviewer must approve before the period is locked.

Who can close a period?

Strata managers, the building chairperson, and super admins can initiate a reconciliation close. EC members can approve a discrepancy but cannot initiate the close.

Step 1 — Initiate the close

  1. Navigate to Finances → Reconciliation in the main menu.
  2. Select the period you wish to close (e.g. "April 2026").
  3. Click Close Period and enter the bank statement total.
  4. The system will run a reconciliation check automatically.

Step 2a — Balanced: automatic close

If the journal entry total equals the bank statement total, the period transitions immediately to Closed status. No further action is required. You will receive a confirmation notification with the closing timestamp.

Step 2b — Discrepancy detected: reviewer approval required

If the totals do not match, the system will:

  1. Display the discrepancy amount (journal total vs bank statement total).
  2. Email the nominated reviewer (usually the strata manager) with the discrepancy details.
  3. Hold the period in Pending Review status until a reviewer approves.

The reviewer will receive an email with a link to the reconciliation summary. Once they have investigated and are satisfied with the explanation, they click Approve and Close in the portal, optionally adding review notes.

Note: The period will not close until the reviewer explicitly approves, regardless of how much time passes. There is no automatic timeout — this is by design to protect the financial record.

What happens after close?

  • The period status changes to Closed and no further transactions can be posted to it.
  • A Merkle seal is computed for the period's ledger, providing tamper-evident proof of the closing balance.
  • The closing balance becomes the opening balance for the next period.
  • A confirmation email is sent to the strata manager and chairperson.

Common questions

What if I entered the wrong bank statement total?

Contact your strata manager to void the reconciliation request and start a new one. Closed periods cannot be re-opened, but an open-period reconciliation that is pending review can be cancelled.

How long does the reviewer have to respond?

There is no automatic expiry. The period stays in Pending Review status until the reviewer approves or the reconciliation request is cancelled.

Can I see the discrepancy details?

Yes. The reconciliation summary page shows the journal entry total, the bank statement total, and the discrepancy amount. Strata managers and the chairperson can view this at any time while the period is in Pending Review.

Is reconciliation available for all buildings?

The automated reconciliation close workflow is available for buildings where the Financial Integration Layer v2 feature is enabled. Contact your strata manager if you are unsure whether this is active for your building.
