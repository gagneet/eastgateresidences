Jurisdictional Compliance Rules

Each strata building operates under the legislation of the state or territory where it is located. This guide explains what rules apply to your building and how the platform enforces them automatically.

Access

The Jurisdictional Rules page is available to strata managers, chairpersons, and super admins. Navigate to Admin → Jurisdictional Rules in the dashboard.

Your Building's Jurisdiction

When your building was onboarded, it was assigned to the legislation of its state or territory:

  • ACT — Unit Titles (Management) Act 2011
  • NSW — Strata Schemes Management Act 2015
  • VIC — Owners Corporations Act 2006
  • QLD — Body Corporate and Community Management Act 1997

The platform applies the rules for your jurisdiction automatically to every financial write, levy notice, interest calculation, and fund transfer.

Levy Interest

When levies are overdue the platform calculates interest according to your jurisdiction's statutory maximum rate. For example:

  • ACT — default 10% p.a., maximum 20% p.a. by special resolution (UTMA 2011 s.76)
  • NSW — up to 10% p.a. (SSMA 2015 s.85); a 30-day grace period applies before interest accrues
  • QLD — up to 30% p.a. effective ceiling (BCCM Act; 2.5% per month)

No interest is charged until the grace period for your jurisdiction has elapsed.

Fund Transfers

Some jurisdictions restrict transfers between the administrative fund and the sinking (capital works) fund:

  • ACT / NSW / VIC — transfers are permitted with an ordinary or special resolution
  • QLD — transfers between funds are prohibited (BCCM Standard Module reg 100(7))

Attempting a fund transfer for a QLD building will be blocked with a clear error message.

Budget Cap and Committee Spending

NSW buildings must obtain a general meeting resolution before approving invoices that would push any budget line more than 10% above its approved amount (SSMA s.102). The platform checks this automatically when you approve an AP invoice.

QLD buildings apply a committee spending cap of $200 per lot for each item of expenditure (BCCM Standard Module reg 100). Large expenditure items must be approved by an ordinary resolution.

Trust and Pooled Accounts

Whether a strata manager may hold scheme funds in a pooled trust account depends on jurisdiction:

  • ACT — pooled trust is permitted (Agents Act 2003 Part 7)
  • NSW — permitted; interest accrues to a statutory account, not the scheme
  • VIC — pooled trust is not permitted (OCA s.122)
  • QLD — not permitted; each body corporate requires a separate trust account

Levy Notice Hardship Statement

NSW legislation requires that every levy notice include a statement about hardship assistance (SSMA 2015 s.89). The platform adds this statement automatically to all levy notice PDFs for NSW buildings.

Audits and Trial Balance

The deadline for lodging the annual trial balance and completing the financial audit differs by jurisdiction:

  • ACT — trial balance due within 15 days of AGM; audit by 30 September
  • NSW — trial balance due within 21 days of AGM (PSA Regulations 2022 reg 30)
  • VIC — audit must be completed before the AGM

Settlement Adjustment (Conveyancing)

When a lot is sold, the current levy period is split between the outgoing and incoming owner based on the settlement date. Strata managers and EC members can generate a settlement adjustment calculation at Admin → Settlement Adjustment.

Enter the lot number and settlement date. The platform calculates:

  • How many days of the levy period fall before settlement (outgoing owner's share)
  • How many days fall on or after settlement (incoming owner's share)
  • The levy amount in dollars for each party

This calculation uses the active levy period for your building and is automatically pro-rated to the cent.

Viewing All Jurisdiction Rules

The All Jurisdictions tab on the Jurisdictional Rules page lets you compare rules across all supported states and territories. This is useful when onboarding a new building or advising owners who own property in multiple states.

Per-Building Overrides

In rare cases, a building may have an override applied by a super admin — for example, a custom interest rate agreed by special resolution. The Full Rules table shows the effective value (including any overrides) alongside the statutory citation.
