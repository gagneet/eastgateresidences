# Building Financial Stress Score Guide
## For Owners, EC Members, and Strata Managers

---

## What is the Stress Score?

The Building Financial Stress Score is a single number from **0 to 100** that summarises
the financial health of your Owners Corporation.

**Lower is better:**

| Score    | Category    | What it means                        |
|----------|-------------|--------------------------------------|
| 0 – 25   | 🟢 Healthy  | Building is financially sound        |
| 26 – 50  | 🟡 Watch    | Some indicators need monitoring      |
| 51 – 75  | 🟠 Elevated | Significant financial pressure       |
| 76 – 100 | 🔴 Critical | Urgent financial intervention needed |

---

## What Goes Into the Score?

The score combines **7 components**, each with a different weight. All seven are
evaluated and combined using a weighted average to produce the final score.

| Component             | Weight | What it measures                                                    |
|-----------------------|--------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Reserve Adequacy      | 25%    | Is the sinking fund large enough for planned capital works?         |
| Arrears Level         | 20%    | What percentage of levies are outstanding across all units?         |
| Arrears Concentration | 15%    | Is one owner responsible for most of the total arrears debt?        |
| Unreconciled Exposure | 15%    | How much cash is unmatched in the bank reconciliation?              |
| Maintenance Pressure  | 10%    | How many building assets are in high-risk or critical condition?    |
| Liquidity Coverage    | 10%    | Does the admin fund have enough cash to cover 3 months of expenses? |
| Capital Shock Risk    | 5%     | What is the probability of an unexpected large capital expenditure? |

### Score Category Thresholds

| Score Range | Category | Indicator                            |
|-------------|----------|--------------------------------------|
| 0 – 25      | Healthy  | Building is financially sound        |
| 26 – 50     | Watch    | Some indicators need monitoring      |
| 51 – 75     | Elevated | Significant financial pressure       |
| 76 – 100    | Critical | Urgent financial intervention needed |

### Graceful Degradation

If data for one or more components is unavailable (for example, no capital works plan
has been entered, or reconciliation has not been completed), those components are
**excluded from the calculation** and their weights are redistributed proportionally
across the available components.

A **"Partial Data"** notice appears on the dashboard when this applies. The score is
still valid and actionable, but indicates that full accuracy requires the missing data
to be entered. Contact your Strata Manager to ensure all data sources are up to date.

Components most commonly missing in new buildings:

- Capital Shock Risk (requires capital works plan)
- Unreconciled Exposure (requires completed bank reconciliation)
- Maintenance Pressure (requires asset condition assessments)

## What to Do at Each Category

### 🟢 Healthy (0–25)

Your building is in good financial shape. Maintain current levy levels and continue
regular maintenance schedules.

**Actions:** Review at next AGM. No urgent action required.

### 🟡 Watch (26–50)

One or more indicators are heading in the wrong direction. This is not an emergency,
but your EC should investigate the specific components contributing to the score.

**Actions:**

- Review the component breakdown to identify which signals are elevated
- Consider whether levy increases are needed at the next AGM
- Ensure outstanding arrears are being actively followed up

### 🟠 Elevated (51–75)

Significant financial pressure exists. The EC should treat this as a priority item.

**Actions:**

- Commission a financial review
- Contact owners with outstanding arrears
- Review the capital works plan for any items that can be deferred
- Consider a special levy if the sinking fund is critically low

### 🔴 Critical (76–100)

The building faces urgent financial risk. Immediate EC and Strata Manager action is required.

**Actions:**

- Convene an extraordinary general meeting
- Engage a financial specialist
- Notify owners of the situation and proposed remediation
- Consider emergency levy if legally permitted

- A plain-English explanation

## Data Completeness

If the score shows a **"Partial Data"** notice, it means one or more of the 7 components
could not be calculated due to missing data. The remaining components are re-weighted
proportionally so the score still falls in the 0–100 range. The score is valid for
operational decisions but should be treated as indicative until all data is available.

See the "Graceful Degradation" section under "What Goes Into the Score?" for details on
which components are most commonly absent in new or partially configured buildings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often is the score updated?**
A: The score is computed live each time you visit the dashboard.

**Q: Can I see the score history?**
A: Yes. The trend indicator (↑ Improving / → Stable / ↓ Deteriorating) shows whether
the score has changed from the previous quarter.

**Q: Who can see the stress score?**
A: EC members, Chairman, and Strata Manager can see the full breakdown.
Owners can see the overall category (Healthy/Watch/Elevated/Critical).
