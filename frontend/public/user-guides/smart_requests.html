Smart Requests

Submit queries and requests to management — with instant answers for common questions.

What Are Smart Requests?

Smart Requests is the formal channel for residents to submit questions, concerns, and requests to building management. Instead of sending untracked emails, every request is logged, assigned, and tracked through a structured workflow.

The system includes an auto-resolution layer: for common query types (such as a levy balance inquiry or a by-law question), the platform can answer instantly without involving management — saving time on both sides.

Navigate to: Dashboard → Requests

Who can use Smart Requests? All residents — owners, tenants, and guests — can submit requests. Management roles (EC members, governance chairs, strata managers, and strata admins) process and resolve incoming requests.

Request Categories

When submitting a request, choose the category that best matches your query. The category determines how the request is routed and which auto-resolution rules apply.

Category Use it for Auto-Resolution
Maintenance Reporting a fault or repair need in common areas (e.g. broken light, lift issue, damaged fence). Unit-specific maintenance goes through the Maintenance module — this category is for general building concerns. No — routes directly to manager
Levy Query Questions about your levy amount, payment due date, credit balance, or discrepancy on a levy notice. Yes — your current balance, next due date, and levy breakdown are shown instantly based on your unit's ledger
By-Law Query Asking about what is or is not permitted under the strata by-laws — e.g. keeping a pet, renovating, parking rules, noise hours. Yes — common by-law questions are answered from the by-laws knowledge base; unusual questions escalate to management
Document Request Requesting a copy of a building document — meeting minutes, insurance certificate, by-laws PDF, levy notice, or any other official document. Partial — documents already uploaded in the Documents module are linked automatically; unavailable documents escalate to management
Noise Complaint Reporting persistent or unreasonable noise from another unit or common area. All noise complaints are handled confidentially. No — always reviewed by management; complainant identity is protected
Other Any query that does not fit the above categories. The manager will triage and categorise it on receipt. No — reviewed by manager

Workflow Stages

Every request moves through a defined pipeline from submission to resolution. You can track the current stage at any time from the My Requests list.

1

Open

The request has been submitted and is waiting to be picked up by management. If auto-resolution is triggered, the request may skip directly to Auto-Resolved without entering the manual workflow.

2

In Review

A manager or EC member has opened the request and is reviewing it. You may receive a clarifying question at this stage — respond via the request thread.

3

In Progress

Action is being taken. This could mean a contractor has been contacted (for Maintenance), a document is being prepared (for Document Requests), or a formal by-law notice is being drafted (for Noise Complaints).

4

Resolved

Management has addressed the request and provided a response or outcome. You will receive an in-app notification and email when your request is resolved. Review the resolution — if you are satisfied, no action is needed. If the issue persists, you can re-open the request.

5

Closed

The request is complete and archived. Closed requests are visible in your request history for 12 months. Closed requests cannot be re-opened — submit a new request if the issue recurs.

⚡

Auto-Resolved

The system answered your query instantly using building data or the by-laws knowledge base. The answer is shown immediately on screen. No management action is required. Auto-resolved requests are still recorded in your history.

Typical response times: Auto-Resolved requests are instant. Management aims to respond to all other requests within 2 business days. Noise Complaints and urgent Maintenance requests are prioritised.

How to Submit a Request

Step 1 — Choose your category: Navigate to Dashboard → Requests and click New Request. Select the category that best matches your query from the dropdown. The form adapts to show fields relevant to that category.
Step 2 — Fill in the form: Enter a short subject line and a detailed description. Be specific — include unit numbers, dates, or prior correspondence where relevant. For Maintenance requests, describe the fault location and how long the issue has existed. For Noise Complaints, include the approximate times the noise occurs.
Step 3 — Submit and track: Click Submit Request. If auto-resolution is triggered, your answer appears immediately on screen. Otherwise, the request appears in your My Requests list with status Open and you will receive updates by notification as it progresses.
Emergencies: Smart Requests is not for emergencies. If there is a fire, flood, gas leak, or immediate safety risk, call 000 immediately, then contact your building's emergency contact line. Emergency contacts are listed under Dashboard → Emergency Services.

How to Track a Request

All your submitted requests are listed under Dashboard → Requests → My Requests, sorted by most recent activity.

Reading the Request Timeline

Click any request in your list to open the detail view. The Timeline tab shows the full history of the request:

  • Submitted — when you created the request, with the original text
  • Status changes — each transition (e.g. Open → In Review) timestamped with the manager's name
  • Notes from management — any updates or questions posted to the request thread
  • Your replies — any clarifications you provided
  • Resolution note — the outcome recorded when the request was resolved

Replying to a Request

If management asks a clarifying question, you will receive an in-app notification. Open the request and type your reply in the Reply box at the bottom of the timeline. Click Send Reply. The manager is notified of your response automatically.

Re-Opening a Resolved Request

If a resolved request is not actually fixed, click Re-open Request in the detail view. This returns the request to Open status with a note that it was re-opened. Provide a reason so management understands what was not adequately addressed.

Auto-Resolution: How It Works

Smart Requests can answer certain query types instantly without involving management. Auto-resolution is triggered when the system determines it has enough data to provide a definitive answer.

Levy Query Auto-Resolution

When you submit a Levy Query, the system immediately checks your unit's levy ledger and returns:

  • Your current outstanding balance (or credit)
  • Your next levy due date and the amount due
  • A breakdown of your levy by fund (Admin / Sinking)
  • A link to your full levy history

By-Law Query Auto-Resolution

For common by-law topics (pets, noise, parking, renovations, short-term letting), the system searches the by-laws knowledge base and returns the relevant clause with a plain-English summary. If the query matches no known topic, it escalates to management for a manual answer.

Auto-resolution is read-only: The system provides information only. It does not make decisions, grant approvals, or waive any obligation. For anything requiring a formal decision (e.g. approval to renovate, permission to keep a pet), your request will proceed to a manager regardless of auto-resolution status.

Manager Queue (EC / Chairman / Strata Manager / Admin)

Management roles have a dedicated Manager Queue view accessible via the toggle button at the top of the Smart Requests page. This queue shows all building requests, not just your own.

Triage Stats Dashboard

At the top of the manager queue, three live stat cards show:

  • Auto-Resolved Today — requests resolved by the AI assistant without human intervention.
  • Deflection Rate — percentage of requests resolved without escalation (target > 60%).
  • Received This Week — total new requests in the current week across all categories.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my request was received?

After submitting, the request immediately appears in your My Requests list with status Open, and you receive an in-app confirmation notification. You will also receive a confirmation email to your registered address.

Can I attach a photo or document?

Yes. When filling in the request form, use the Attach File button to upload photos, PDFs, or other supporting documents (max 10 MB per attachment). Attachments are especially useful for Maintenance requests (photos of the fault) and Noise Complaints (if you have recorded evidence).

How long before I get a response?

Auto-resolved requests are instant. For all others, management aims to respond within 2 business days. You can see the current status at any time in your request list. If your request has been in Open status for more than 5 business days, you will receive an escalation notice.

Will my noise complaint be kept confidential?

Yes. Noise Complaints are visible only to management. Your name and unit are not disclosed to the subject of the complaint. Any formal by-law notice issued as a result refers only to observed conduct, not the source of the complaint.

I submitted a Levy Query but the auto-answer looks wrong — what do I do?

Add a reply to the request explaining what appears incorrect. This flags the request for manual review by your strata manager, who will verify the ledger and respond with a corrected answer.
