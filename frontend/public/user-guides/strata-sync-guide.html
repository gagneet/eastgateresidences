Financial Data Sync — Admin Guide

This guide explains how to get financial data into the portal and keep it up to date. It covers both the automated portal sync and the manual CSV upload path, and explains how each source feeds the finance pages visible to committee members and owners.

Audience: Strata Manager Chairman Super Admin
EC Members can view sync status but cannot trigger a sync or upload data.

1. Overview

There are two ways to bring financial data into the portal:

Method When to use Page
Sync from the strata management portal (Civium) Monthly, after the strata manager releases the committee report. Pulls live bank balances, levy positions, and budget actuals automatically. /dashboard/admin/strata-sync
Upload a CSV file manually When you have an AGM-ratified levy schedule, a corrected annual levy file, or data that the portal does not expose via its sync interface. /dashboard/admin/financial-data

Both methods update the same underlying financial collections used by the levy collection rate, fund health, and owner balance pages. If both sources exist for the same financial year, the CSV/AGM-ratified data takes precedence over portal sync data.

2. Syncing from the Portal

Page: /dashboard/admin/strata-sync

The portal sync connects to your strata management software (Civium) and pulls the latest financial snapshot into the portal database. No data is sent back — it is a read-only pull.

Step-by-step

  1. 1 Navigate to Admin → Strata Sync in the dashboard sidebar.
  2. 2 Click Start Sync. A browser window will open prompting you to log in to the Civium portal with your strata manager credentials.
  3. 3 Complete the two-factor authentication when prompted — enter your 2FA PIN or approve the push notification.
  4. 4 The sync runs in the background. A progress indicator shows which data categories are being fetched (bank accounts, levy positions, spending categories).
  5. 5 When the sync completes, a preview panel shows a summary of what was retrieved. Review the figures — spot-check the bank balances and total levy position against the committee report you have on hand.
  6. 6 Click Confirm to write the data into the portal database. Click Discard if the figures look incorrect.

What data is populated by a sync

Data Category Where it appears in the portal
Admin fund and sinking fund bank account balances Finance Overview → Bank Account Balances card
Budget vs actual spending by category Finance → Charts tab → Spending Categories; Budget vs Actual pages
Owner levy positions (arrears, credits, current balance per unit) Collection Rate page → Civium Portal Snapshot; Unit Finance Detail → Portal Cross-Check
Portal-reported collection rate and risk level Collection Rate page → Civium Portal Snapshot

After the sync

Once confirmed, the following pages automatically reflect the updated portal data:

  • Collection Rate — portal arrears total and collection rate percentage update immediately
  • Fund Health — bank balances feed the health score calculation
  • Arrears Board — unit-level portal balances update the cross-check column
  • Budget vs Actual — the "Portal Actual" column updates with the latest spending figures
Finance cutover note. The same Civium snapshot can also be copied into a review-only staging record for PostgreSQL validation. That background review does not change the live finance screens, levy notices, or payment history; MongoDB remains the live finance source until the shadow-read checks are approved.

Recommended sync frequency

Sync monthly, after your strata manager distributes the monthly committee report. The report and the portal data will then be in agreement, and any discrepancies visible on the portal will be genuine outstanding items rather than timing differences.

3. Uploading CSV Financial Data

Page: /dashboard/admin/financial-data

The CSV upload path is designed for data that originates outside the portal — typically the annual levy schedule ratified at the AGM, or a corrected ledger export from your accounting system.

Step-by-step

  1. 1 Navigate to Admin → Financial Data in the dashboard sidebar.
  2. 2 Select the financial year the file covers from the year dropdown.
  3. 3 Click Choose File and select the CSV exported from your accounting system. The file must match the required column format (see below).
  4. 4 Click Upload. The system validates the file and reports any column or format errors before writing any data.
  5. 5 Review the validation summary — it shows total records found, any units not matched to the building roll, and the total levy figures parsed.
  6. 6 Click Confirm Import to write the data.

Required CSV columns

Column Description Example
year Financial year as a 4-digit number 2026
admin_fund_income Total admin fund income budgeted for the year (ex-GST, dollars) 309882.00
admin_fund_expenses Total admin fund expenses budgeted for the year (ex-GST, dollars) 301500.00
sinking_fund_income Total sinking fund levy income budgeted (ex-GST, dollars) 90459.00
sinking_fund_expenses Total sinking fund expenditure budgeted (ex-GST, dollars) 45000.00
unit_number Unit identifier matching the building roll (one row per unit) UA001, TH012
annual_levy Annual levy amount for the unit (GST-inclusive, dollars) 6121.20
Important: Uploaded data is marked as "authoritative" — it will not be overwritten by future portal syncs. If you need to correct an uploaded figure, upload a new file for the same year; the new upload replaces the previous one.

4. How Data Flows to Finance Pages

Understanding which source feeds which page helps you diagnose missing or stale figures.

Source Database collection(s) written Finance pages affected
Portal sync (Civium) levy_categories Fund Health, Budget vs Actual, Spending Categories chart
Portal sync (Civium) unit_levy_ledger (portal cross-check fields) Collection Rate → Civium Portal Snapshot, Unit Finance Detail → Portal Cross-Check
Portal sync (Civium) bank_accounts Finance Overview → Bank Account Balances card
CSV upload annual_levies Levy Calculator, Owner Finance pages, Budget totals
CSV upload unit_levy_ledger (authoritative levy schedule) Collection Rate, Owner Balances, Levy Status tab
Precedence rule: If both a portal sync and a CSV upload exist for the same financial year, the CSV/AGM-ratified levy schedule takes precedence for billing figures (total levied, unit levy amounts). Portal data is still shown in its dedicated cross-check columns and is never deleted.

5. First-Time Setup / Data Not Appearing After Sync

If the finance pages show no data even though the Strata Sync page reports a successful sync, it usually means the one-time data migration has not yet been run for your building.

Why this happens

Syncs performed before the finance bridge was installed wrote data to the portal's raw sync tables but did not copy it into the collections used by the finance dashboard. This is a one-off gap that only affects buildings where syncs pre-date the bridge installation.

How to resolve it

  1. Contact your system administrator and ask them to run the one-time data migration for your building.
  2. Once the migration is complete, all historical sync data will appear on the finance pages.
  3. All subsequent syncs will automatically write to both places — no further migration is needed.
After migration: Data from past syncs will back-fill into the finance pages. You do not need to re-run those syncs.

Different cause: your building requires manual review before every sync (added 2026-08-19)

A small number of buildings (East Gate is currently the only one) are configured so that a portal sync never automatically updates the category totals shown on finance pages, even for a brand-new sync. This is deliberate, not a bug — for these buildings, a raw scrape is treated as evidence, not fact, and must pass a human review step before it can change a number a dashboard displays.

If your building is one of these, every successful sync still updates the Strata Sync page's own preview and the raw owner-balance data, but the Finance page's category actuals will only change after your system administrator has reviewed the sync's proposed changes and explicitly approved them. Ask your administrator whether your building has this setting enabled if you're unsure why a sync's numbers aren't reflected on the Finance page.

7. Troubleshooting

Symptom Likely cause Action
Collection Rate page shows no data after a successful sync One-time data migration has not been run Ask your system administrator to run the one-time data migration for your building (see Section 5)
Sync shows an authentication error or times out Portal credentials have changed or the session expired mid-sync Go to Settings → Portal Credentials and re-enter the Civium login details, then retry the sync
Sync completes but bank balances show as $0.00 or blank The portal did not return bank account data — can occur when the strata manager has not yet posted the monthly statement Wait until the monthly statement is posted in Civium (usually within 5 business days of month-end), then re-run the sync
Uploaded CSV not reflected on Levy Calculator or Owner Finance pages The year in the CSV does not match the currently selected financial year, or the file failed validation silently Check the year dropdown on the Financial Data page matches the year field in your CSV; re-upload if needed
Unit amounts in the CSV show as "unmatched" The unit_number values in the CSV do not match the building roll Check unit identifiers in the CSV against Admin → Units — ensure formatting matches exactly (e.g. UA001 not U001)
Portal Snapshot figures disagree with the levy ledger Portal data and levy ledger are separate sources; a small difference is expected (timing, unprocessed payments) Large discrepancies (>5%) should be investigated with your strata manager. Small differences are normal and visible in the cross-check column for transparency.
