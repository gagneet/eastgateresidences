📦 Parcel Management

Track deliveries, receive notifications, and manage parcel collection at your building.

Overview

The Parcel Management feature allows admin staff and service providers to log incoming deliveries, and automatically notifies all active residents of a unit the moment their parcel arrives.

From Session 28+, the notification system sends:

  • In-app bell notification — shown in the notification centre immediately
  • Email — sent to both your portal login email and your @ community email (if configured)
  • Live push — via SSE (Server-Sent Events) to any connected browser tab

Logging a Parcel (Admin Staff / Service Provider)

Step 1: Navigate to Dashboard → Parcels.
Step 2: Click Log New Parcel and enter the unit number and carrier.
Step 3: Optionally add a description and tracking number.
Step 4: Click Save. Residents are notified automatically.

Pre-Registering an Expected Parcel (Owners / Residents)

Owners, EC Members, and Tenants can now self-register an expected delivery before it arrives. This helps admin staff identify your parcel quickly.

Step 1: Navigate to Dashboard → Parcels.
Step 2: Click Register Expected Parcel.
Step 3: Select your carrier and optionally enter a tracking number and description. Your unit is pre-filled — you cannot register for another unit.
Step 4: Click Save. Your parcel appears with status Expected.
Step 5: When the parcel arrives, admin staff mark it as Received, and you are notified via bell and email.

Notification Details (Residents)

In-App Notification

A bell 🔔 appears in the top-right corner. Click it to see parcel details and a link to the Parcels page.

Email Notification

You receive an email at both your portal login address and your @ email (if configured). Duplicate addresses are deduplicated — you receive only one email per address.

Live Push (SSE)

If you are logged in to the portal in a browser tab, a real-time parcel_logged event is pushed to your tab. The parcels page updates without needing a manual refresh.

Marking a Parcel as Collected

Step 1: Navigate to Dashboard → Parcels.
Step 2: Find the parcel in the list (status: received).
Step 3: Click Mark Collected. Status changes to collected.

Tracking Parcel Status

If a tracking number was recorded, you can click Track Parcel to see live courier status (where supported) or a simulated status for demo/offline use.

Frequently Asked Questions

I didn't receive an email notification — why?

  • Check your spam/junk folder.
  • Verify your email address is correct in My Profile.
  • Ask your strata manager to confirm your unit is correctly linked to your portal account.

Can I opt out of parcel email notifications?

Yes. Visit Dashboard → Email Preferences and disable parcel notifications.

Who receives notifications if a unit has two owners?

All active owners and tenants linked to the unit receive notifications.
