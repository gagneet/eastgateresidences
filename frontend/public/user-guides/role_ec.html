EC Member Resident Handbook

As an Executive Committee (EC) member, you have additional responsibilities and access levels to oversee the building's operations and finances.

EC Responsibilities
  • Review and approve financial invoices in the Approval Queue.
  • Monitor building Compliance and Maintenance.
  • Collaborate on governance decisions through Committee Meetings.

Management & Approvals

  • Approval Queue: Review pending invoices and financial requests. Your approval ensures vendors are paid promptly and accurately.
  • User Directory: View the full list of registered users and their roles to maintain oversight of who has access to the platform.
  • Audit Logs: Review system activity to ensure transparency and accountability.

Governance

  • Compliance Checklist: Stay on top of statutory requirements such as Fire Safety, Lift Inspections, and Insurance renewals.
  • Action Items (Todos): Manage and track tasks assigned during committee meetings.
  • Service Schedule: Monitor the schedule of recurring maintenance and inspections (e.g., cleaning, landscaping).

Financial Oversight

  • My Property View: Interactive building-wide status and management dashboard.
  • Financial Intelligence: Advanced data analytics for building expenditure and revenue.
  • Budget Projections: View future financial forecasts to assist in long-term planning and levy setting.
  • Financial Overview: Access detailed reports on fund balances and spending trends.
  • Utilities Tracking: Monitor water and council rate costs to identify potential savings or issues (like leaks).
  • Unit Plan: Access official registered units plan, floor plans, and unit entitlements.
Note: You also have full access to all Owner features, including your own property's levy status and facility bookings.

Communication

  • Official Announcements: Draft and post announcements that are broadcasted to all residents.
  • Emergency Contacts: Manage the list of emergency service providers for the building.

Advanced Financial Enhancements

EC Members have view access to the following advanced financial tools.

Arrears Recovery Board (View Only)

View the Debt Recovery Board at Dashboard → Debt Recovery Board to monitor the arrears status of all units. Units are classified across four severity tiers:

  • Monitor — Balance owing, no action yet taken.
  • Contacted — Section 83 notice has been sent (within 14 days).
  • DCA Eligible — More than $1,500 owing and 14+ days have elapsed since the notice.
  • DCA Referred — Formally referred to a Debt Collection Agency.
View Only: EC Members can monitor the Arrears Recovery Board but cannot generate Section 83 Notices or initiate DCA referrals. Those actions require a chairperson appointment with the relevant permission flags, Strata Manager access, or Super Admin access.

Levy Fairness Analysis

Navigate to Dashboard → Intelligence → Levy Fairness for a 5-tab interactive analysis of levy equity across all lots.

  • Overview: LBFI score (80.8 = Good), Subsidy Map showing which facilities drive cross-subsidisation, and a Scenario Comparison chart (current UOE model vs. benefit-based model).
  • Facilities: Edit facility tiers (Global/Apartments Only/Townhouses Only) and override annual costs. Add manual items. Click Regenerate to refresh all results.
  • Unit Impact: Per-lot table showing current levy vs. benefit-based levy, dollar change, and percentage change. Sortable and searchable.
  • Budget Tiers: 3-tier cost breakdown with donut chart and Levy Fairness equity audit figures.
  • AGM Report: Download a professional A4 PDF for owner distribution — includes LBFI gauge, bar charts, per-unit impact table, and legal implementation pathway.

EC Member Edit Access

EC Members can now directly edit the capital works plan from the UI. Click Edit Plan to enter edit mode on any of the three tabs:

  • Sinking Fund Plan (2021–2035): Edit contribution and expenditure amounts per year; opening/closing balances recompute automatically client-side before saving.
  • Capital Event Milestones: Add, edit, or delete major works events (year, label, amount, severity, description).
  • 10-Year Replacement Schedule: Add manual replacement items or modify auto-generated ones. Manual edits persist until the next engine sync.
Note: The Sync Engine button (rebuilds the replacement schedule from asset lifespans) is available to EC Members, Chairmen, Strata Managers, and Super Admins.

Global Building Risk Index

The Building Risk Index provides a single composite 0–100 risk score for the building, combining five weighted components. A lower score is better.

Score Risk Level Meaning
0–24 LOW Building is stable with no immediate concerns
25–49 MODERATE Some areas to monitor — routine attention required
50–74 HIGH Action plan required — EC should prioritise
75–100 CRITICAL Urgent intervention — possible special levy risk

Risk Score Components

  • Asset Health (25%): Average condition of all tracked building assets (lifts, fire systems, etc.)
  • Financial Stability (20%): Budget surplus/deficit forecast and financial reserve outlook
  • Reserve Adequacy (20%): Sinking fund balance vs 10-year capital replacement forecast
  • Capital Shock Risk (20%): Risk of large unplanned capital expenditure events
  • Maintenance Risk (15%): Open/overdue maintenance requests and resolution trends

Where to Find It

  • Dashboard widget: Visible on your Property Dashboard as an EC Member
  • Intelligence Hub: Dashboard → Intelligence → Risk Index
  • Public Building Report: your building's public report page — a PII-free public summary suitable for prospective buyers and real estate agents

Refreshing the Score

The risk score is computed live from the database when you load the page. EC Members can view the score; the appointed chairperson can force a refresh when the relevant permission flag is active, which also logs the action in the audit trail.

Financial Health Score

View the building's Financial Health Score (0–100) on the Financial Intelligence page. The score is calculated from seven components: Surplus Ratio, Arrears Percentage, Cashflow Buffer, Forecast Stability, Budget Discipline, Expense Volatility, and Reserve Adequacy.

True Cost of Ownership

View a per-unit cost breakdown at Finance → True Cost of Ownership, showing admin levies, sinking fund contributions, special levies, council rates, and water charges for each lot. This assists in understanding the full financial burden on each owner when preparing for budget discussions.

Tax Summary PDF

Download an annual levy breakdown PDF for your own unit — useful for tax return purposes on investment properties. Navigate to Finance → Levy Status and select Download Tax Summary.

OwnerHub — Landlord & Property Management

As an EC Member (who also owns a unit), you have full access to OwnerHub — the same landlord platform available to regular owners, plus an admin-level view of all properties in the building.

OwnerHub Overview

Navigate to OwnerHub → Overview for a consolidated view of all registered properties, active tenancies, upcoming inspections, and rent ledger summary across the building.

My Properties

EC Members can register and manage their own investment property details (address, purchase price, mortgage) and view all other properties in the building for governance purposes. Navigate to OwnerHub → My Properties.

Tenancies & Leases

View all active and historical tenancy agreements for your own property and browse building-wide tenancy data for governance oversight. Navigate to OwnerHub → Tenancies & Leases.

Rent Ledger

Track rental payment history for your property. EC Members also have read-only visibility of all rent ledgers for governance reporting. Navigate to OwnerHub → Rent Ledger.

Inspections

Schedule and record property inspections for your unit. As an EC Member, you can also view all outstanding inspections across the building. Navigate to OwnerHub → Inspections.

Building Financial Stress

Access the Building Financial Stress dashboard at Building Financial Stress in the sidebar. This is now fully scoped to the selected building and shows reserve adequacy, risk scores, and mitigation options specific to the building you are managing.

Role Inheritance: EC Members and Chairmen inherit all owner-level privileges in addition to their management access. If you step down from the EC, your account will revert to a standard Owner role and you will retain access to all owner features through OwnerHub.

RBAC Permission Model (Session 63)

The platform now enforces fine-grained RBAC + ABAC permission slugs on every request. As EC Member you hold:

Slug Capability
financial.view View building financials and reports
financial.export Export financial data (PDF, CSV)
committee.vote Vote on committee motions
workorder.create / workorder.update Raise and update maintenance work orders
workorder.review Review contractor reports
documents.upload / documents.view Upload and view all building documents
building.view View building profile
user.view View resident and owner records

All permissions are scoped to your building. If you are appointed chairperson, the platform may grant additional slugs such as financial.approve_invoice, committee.finalize_minutes, and workorder.approve for the appointment period. No separate login role is required.

EC Chairperson Responsibilities

Chairperson is an EC position on an ec_member account, not a standalone login role. When you are appointed chairperson, the platform may grant additional governance permissions for the appointment period while keeping your base role as EC Member.

  • Meeting leadership: Set agendas, lead committee meetings, and finalise minutes where your permission flags allow it.
  • Governance approvals: Review high-impact notices, expenditure approvals, and work-order approvals that require committee or chairperson action.
  • Financial oversight: Review arrears, levy fairness, capital works, and financial health reports for committee decision-making.
  • Documents: Manage EC or chairperson-sensitive document access levels when enabled by policy.
  • Escalation: Escalate organisation administration, protected feature toggles, or platform-wide configuration issues to Strata Admin or Super Admin rather than treating them as chairperson-only actions.
Term handling: Chairperson permissions should follow the EC appointment term. When the term ends, chairperson-specific permissions can be revoked while preserving standard EC Member and Owner access.

Role Lifecycle

EC Member assignments have start and end dates (typically AGM to AGM). When the term expires the system sets the assignment inactive and your account automatically reverts to your base Owner role. You retain all owner access throughout — no data or history is lost.
