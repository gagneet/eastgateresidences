Intelligence Module — Quick Reference Guide

Understanding the Property Intelligence and Financial Analytics tools

All Roles with Intelligence Access

Overview

The Intelligence module provides predictive analytics, risk scoring, and financial health insights for your building Residences. All amounts are displayed in AUD with exactly two decimal places. Non-monetary metrics use up to four decimal places.

Navigation

Each intelligence sub-page has a ← Back to Intelligence Hub button at the top-left to return to the main Intelligence dashboard (/dashboard/intelligence).

Help Tooltips

Every metric and KPI card has a ⓘ help icon. Hover over it to see:

  • How the value is calculated
  • What data sources are used
  • What action to take based on the value

Intelligence Pages

1. Intelligence Hub (/dashboard/intelligence)

The main dashboard showing four signal cards and KPI metrics:

  • Attention Needed — Maintenance risks requiring an immediate decision. Links to Asset Intelligence page.
  • Upcoming Capital Costs — Next major replacement milestone. Click to see the full capital works timeline.
  • Financial Stability — Reserve resilience against unexpected shocks. Links to Capital Risk page.
  • Capital Works Risk — Next major capital shock window. Links to Capital Risk page.

KPI cards at the top of the hub include tooltips explaining each metric:

  • Asset Health Score — Composite 0–100 score from age, repair frequency, and service recency.
  • 12m Maintenance Forecast — AI-predicted spend for the next 12 months.
  • High Risk Assets — Count of assets with risk score above 75.
  • 5y Capital Need — Total capital works estimated for the next 5 years.

2. Asset Intelligence (/dashboard/intelligence/assets)

Shows maintenance risks and asset health scores from the Digital Twin asset registry. Click any row to see full asset details including repair history and AI recommendations.

  • Risk Score — 0-100 likelihood of failure. Above 75 = high priority.
  • Health Score — 0-100 condition rating. Below 50 warrants inspection.
  • Repair Cost (12m) — When repair cost approaches replacement cost, replacement is economical.

3. Capital Shock Risk (/dashboard/intelligence/capital-risk)

Note (Session 59): This report is now fully operational. A data migration added building context (building_id) to all records across ~45 collections, resolving the 500 errors that previously prevented the Capital Shock and Levy Fairness reports from loading.

Identifies reserve shortfalls before major asset replacements. Key metrics:

  • Capital Shock Index (CSI) — Year's capital spend ÷ median historical capital spend. CSI > 1.5 = Major risk.
  • SFAR (Sinking Fund Adequacy Ratio) — (Current Reserve + Future Levies) ÷ Planned Capital Works. Below 1.0 = underfunded.
  • Reserve Projection Chart — 10-year trajectory showing balance before/after capital deductions.

4. Levy Fairness (/dashboard/intelligence/levy-fairness)

Note (Session 59): Fully operational after the building_id data migration. All 87 units are now correctly scoped and the page no longer returns errors.

AI-powered analysis of whether levies are distributed fairly across all lots based on the chosen allocation driver (unit entitlement, equal split, area-weighted, etc.). EC Members can edit allocation models and run scenario comparisons.

5. Capital Works Planner (/dashboard/intelligence/capital-planner)

Displays the 15-year sinking fund capital works schedule seeded from the strata plan. Shows replacement projects by year, priority, and cost estimate. EC Members and above can add, edit, and prioritise capital works items via the PUT endpoint. A ← Back to Intelligence Hub button returns to the hub.

6. Levy Stability Score (/dashboard/intelligence/levy-stability)

Accessible to EC Members, Chairman, Strata Manager, and Super Admin. Provides a composite 0–100 stability score for the levy regime, derived from four weighted components:

  • Reserve Adequacy (35%) — Is the sinking fund target-funded for the next capital shock window?
  • Capital Shock Risk (30%) — Probability and magnitude of a special levy event in the next 5 years.
  • Levy Volatility (20%) — Year-on-year variability in levy increases (lower = more stable).
  • Funding Sufficiency (15%) — Current levy income vs. estimated budget requirement.

The page includes:

  • A visual score ring (green ≥ 75, amber 50–74, red < 50) with composite score
  • Four KPI cards showing each component's score and weight
  • Radar chart comparing baseline vs what-if scenario
  • Per-group breakdown (Apartments vs Townhouses)
  • What-If Modelling (managers only): Test how increasing sinking fund contributions, deferring capital works, or securing a loan would change the stability score
  • LSS Methodology explainer card with calculation details

Use Recompute to recalculate from current database state. Use What-If to explore scenarios without saving changes.

7. Levy Fairness — AGM Report Tab

On the Levy Fairness page (/dashboard/intelligence/levy-fairness), the AGM Report tab generates production-ready documents for the Annual General Meeting:

  • Committee Report (PDF) — 8-section A4 report: Executive Summary, LBFI Gauge, Cross-Subsidy Analysis, Facility Cost Allocation, Predictive Risk (Monte Carlo), Per-Lot Impact Table (first 50 rows), Legal Pathway Options, and Action Plan. Suitable for printing and tabling at AGM.
  • AGM Presentation (PPTX) — 8-slide PowerPoint: Title, Agenda, LBFI Explanation, Cross-Subsidy Analysis, Facility Allocation, Per-Lot Highlights, Predictive Risk, and Legal Pathways + Action Plan.
  • Impact CSV — Spreadsheet of all units with current levy, fair levy, proposed levy, and change amounts.

8. Levy Fairness — Transition Scenario

On the Levy Fairness page, the Scenarios tab provides transition cap sliders. When you click Run Transition Scenario, the page now shows:

  • The number of years to full transition at the selected cap rate
  • Per-group impact table showing projected levy per lot for Years 1–4 and the target amount, with delta amounts colour-coded
  • Exact dollar examples inline (e.g., "A townhouse paying $5,200/yr will increase by at most $X under the % cap")

The Save Snapshot tab (Super Admin only) allows saving the current model state as a named snapshot for comparison or rollback.

Finance Intelligence Link from Arrears

The Debt Recovery Board (/dashboard/arrears) includes a Finance Intelligence → shortcut in the top action bar, linking directly to the Finance Intelligence page for related financial analysis.

Number Formatting Policy

The application enforces consistent number formatting across all pages:

  • Currency amounts — Always displayed with exactly 2 decimal places (e.g., $1,234.56)
  • Ratio/index values — Displayed with up to 2 decimal places (e.g., CSI: 1.75)
  • Percentages — Displayed with 1-2 decimal places (e.g., 23.45%)
  • Large currency totals in charts — Displayed in thousands (e.g., $250k) for readability
