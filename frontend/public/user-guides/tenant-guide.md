## 2. How to Submit a Maintenance Request

1. Navigate to **Maintenance → New Request** (or click the **+** button on your dashboard).
2. Select the request type: **Plumbing, Electrical, Structural, Appliance, Other**.
3. Describe the issue clearly (e.g., "Kitchen tap dripping constantly — unit TH007").
4. Attach photos if available (drag and drop or browse).
5. Click **Submit**.
6. You will receive a reference number and email confirmation.
7. Track the status under **Maintenance → My Requests**. You will be notified when a contractor is assigned and when the
   job is completed.

> For **urgent issues** (water leak, no power, security), call the after-hours number listed on the
`/emergency-services` page.

## 3. How to Volunteer

Tenants are welcome to participate in community volunteer events. Your owner's levy account receives the credit, but
your participation strengthens the community.

1. Navigate to **Community → Volunteer Events**.
2. Find an event that interests you and click **Register**.
3. Confirm your registration.
4. Attend on the day — the Strata Manager will mark attendance.
5. Your participation is recorded in the community activity log.

---

## 4. How to Use the Smart Request Form

Use Smart Request for anything that doesn't fit neatly into a maintenance ticket — for example, a noise complaint, a
parking dispute, or a general question about by-laws.

1. Navigate to **Requests → Smart Request**.
2. **Step 1:** Describe your issue in plain language.
3. **Step 2:** Review the suggested category and priority. Adjust if needed.
4. **Step 3:** Confirm and submit.
5. Your request is routed to the right person (Strata Manager, EC, or admin staff). You will be notified of progress via
   email and in-app notifications.

## 5. Community OS Features

Community OS includes two features that tenants can access: Smart Requests and the Volunteer programme.

### Smart Requests (`/dashboard/smart-request`)

Smart Request is the recommended way to raise any issue that does not fit neatly into a standard maintenance ticket.
Examples include noise complaints, parking disputes, by-law questions, or general strata enquiries.

**How to submit a request:**

1. Navigate to **Requests → Smart Request** from your dashboard.
2. **Step 1 — Describe the issue:** Type a plain-English description (e.g., "Loud music from apartment above me after
   midnight").
3. **Step 2 — Review category and priority:** The system suggests a category (e.g., "Noise Complaint") and a priority
   level. Adjust either if needed.
4. **Step 3 — Confirm and submit:** Check the summary and click **Submit Request**.
5. You will receive a reference number and an email confirmation.
6. Track progress at **Requests → My Requests**. You are notified by email and in-app at each status change.

**Supported request types for tenants:**

- Maintenance issues (common property, intercom, car park, lifts)
- Noise complaints and neighbour disputes
- By-law questions
- General strata or building enquiries
- Parking and visitor access

**Priority SLAs:**

| Priority | Target first response | Target resolution |
|----------|-----------------------|-------------------|
| Critical | 2 hours               | 24 hours          |
| High     | 4 hours               | 72 hours          |
| Medium   | 24 hours              | 7 days            |
| Low      | 72 hours              | 30 days           |

> For **urgent safety issues** (water leak, no power, security breach), call the after-hours number on the
`/emergency-services` page rather than submitting a Smart Request.

### Volunteer Events (`/dashboard/volunteer`)

Tenants are welcome to participate in community volunteer events. Your participation strengthens the community and is
recorded in the community activity log. Because levy accounts are linked to lot owners, the levy credit for your
attendance is applied to your owner's lot account — not directly to you.

**How to register:**

1. Navigate to **Community → Volunteer Events**.
2. Browse upcoming events. Each listing shows the date, time, location, and the levy credit offered.
3. Click **Register** on the event you want to join and confirm in the dialog.
4. Attend on the day. The Strata Manager will mark attendance after the event.
5. Your participation is logged. The owner's levy account receives the credit.

> If your owner has authorised you to act on their behalf regarding levy matters, ask them to confirm this in writing to
> the Strata Manager.

## 6. What You Cannot Do

The following actions are reserved for lot owners under the ACT Owners Corporation Act 2024:

- **Vote on OC proposals** — motions affect levies and common property, which are the legal responsibility of lot
  owners.
- **Access financial records** — levy schedules, budget reports, and annual statements are for owners.
- **Attend AGM as a voting delegate** — tenants may attend as observers only (with EC approval).
- **Submit proxy votes** — proxy voting is an owner right.

If you believe you need access to any of the above, ask your property owner or property manager to act on your behalf.
