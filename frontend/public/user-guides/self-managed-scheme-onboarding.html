Self-Managed Scheme Onboarding

Onboarding an Owners Corporation that runs its own affairs without a Strata Management firm

Who this guide is for

Super admins creating a self-managed scheme on the platform. For Strata-Management-firm onboarding (the standard path), see the Building Onboarding Guide instead.

What "self-managed" means on StrataOS

A self-managed scheme is a strata scheme (Owners Corporation or Body Corporate) that does not engage a Strata Management firm. Examples include smaller blocks where an EC member volunteers to act as the strata administrator, or a Body Corporate that handles its own correspondence and finances.

Self-managed schemes still must comply with all state legislation regarding insurance, maintenance, and meetings — see your jurisdiction's act:

  • ACT — Unit Titles (Management) Act 2011
  • NSW — Strata Schemes Management Act 2015

On StrataOS this is modelled as a single tenant (core.tenants.is_self_managed=TRUE) that owns exactly one scheme. EC members hold platform roles such as strata_admin or strata_manager directly.

How to onboard a self-managed scheme

  1. Sign in as a Super Admin, then go to Admin → SM Organisations.
  2. Click + New Self-Managed Scheme.
  3. Fill in scheme details:
    • Plan number — the unit/strata plan number registered with the land titles office.
    • Jurisdiction — the Australian state or territory in which the scheme is registered.
    • Scheme name — the building's display name (e.g. "East Gate Residences").
    • Legal name (optional) — typically "The Owners — Units Plan {plan_number}".
    • ABN (optional) — if the Owners Corporation has its own ABN. 11 digits, no spaces.
  4. Fill in the EC admin (first user):
    • The first EC member who will manage the scheme on the platform.
    • Choose role: Strata Admin (full administrative access) or Strata Manager.
    • Pick an invitation expiry (7 / 14 / 30 days).
  5. Click Create self-managed scheme. The platform will:
    • Create the tenant (is_self_managed=TRUE) and the scheme atomically.
    • Send an invitation email to the EC admin with an activation link.
    • Redirect you to the new organisation's detail page.
  6. The EC admin clicks the link in their email, sets a password, and signs in. From there they can complete lots, opening fund balances, and further EC-member invitations via Setup my Scheme in their dashboard.

What if something goes wrong?

Error message What it means
An active organisation already has this ABN A non-archived tenant already uses this ABN. If it's the same OC under a different name, archive the old one first.
Scheme '{n}' already exists in {jur} A scheme with this plan number is already registered in this jurisdiction. Verify with the land titles office.
This self-managed tenant already has a scheme Self-managed tenants are 1:1 with schemes. The system will not create a second scheme for the same tenant.
Created, but invitation email failed The tenant + scheme are saved; only email delivery failed. Resend from the organisation's Invitations tab.

Differences from SM-managed onboarding

  • SM-managed orgs (a Strata Management firm) can have many buildings; self-managed tenants own exactly one scheme.
  • The scheme is created at the same time as the tenant — there is no separate "Onboard a Building" wizard for self-managed.
  • Lots, opening fund balances, and further EC member invites are completed by the EC admin, not by the platform Super Admin.
