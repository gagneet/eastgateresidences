Owner Quick Guide

For owners who need documents, levies, maintenance, bookings, meetings/AGM voting, and community tools.

You can do
  • View approved documents
  • Submit and track maintenance requests
  • Book amenities and moving/lift slots (where enabled)
  • Create and manage marketplace listings
  • Use community chat and private messages
  • View meetings and vote in AGM (where enabled)
  • View and pay levies (as configured)
  • Access budget projections and financial intelligence
You cannot
  • Upload/manage document permissions
  • Post official announcements
  • Manage other users or approve registrations
  • Manage building-wide finances

Registration Process

Registering as an owner takes a few minutes. Your account will not be active until an administrator approves it — this protects all residents' data.

  1. Fill out the registration form — Go to the registration page and enter your details. Make sure to select your correct unit/lot number from the dropdown. A mismatch will delay your approval.
  2. Confirmation screen — After submitting, you will see a confirmation screen with a summary of your registration details. You can update your phone number or address directly from this screen while you wait.
  3. Admin approval required — Your account is created in a pending state. An administrator (strata manager, strata admin, or super admin) must approve your account before you can log in. This step is manual and may take up to one business day.
  4. Email notification on approval — Once your account is approved, you will receive an email notification confirming access. You can then log in and access all owner features. If you do not receive an email within 24 hours, contact your strata manager.
Important: If you try to log in before your account is approved, you will see an "account pending approval" message. This is expected — please wait for the approval email.

Levy Status & Calculator

Owners now have direct access to their levy information on the Finance page:

  • Levy Status tab — Shows your unit's current levy position (amount owing, credit, or fully paid). Only your own unit's data is displayed.
  • Levy Calculator tab — Shows your annual levy breakdown for the selected financial year (admin fund + sinking fund). If no levy data is available for a selected year, a clear message is shown instead of a blank screen. The year selector accepts both short format (2026) and financial-year format (2026-27).

How Your Levy Is Calculated

Your annual strata levy is based on your unit's Unit of Entitlement (UOE) — a proportional share assigned when the strata plan was registered. Higher UOE = larger unit = higher levy.

  • Annual levy = UOE × levy rate per UOE (set each year in the annual budget)
  • Typical UOE values: 82 (studio/1-bed), 111 (2-bed), 139 (large 2-bed), 149 (penthouse), 160–161 (townhouse)
  • FY2026–27 total budget: $440,375 (Admin: $340,870 + Sinking: $99,505)

Payment Schedule

Levies are payable in four quarterly instalments. Your building's levy due dates (check financial settings):

  • Q1: 1 March 2026
  • Q2: 1 June 2026
  • Q3: 1 September 2026
  • Q4: 1 December 2026

A 14-day grace period applies after each due date before an amount is considered overdue.

Payments can be made via DEFT (BPAY), bank transfer, or through this portal. DEFT/bank payments are recorded in the strata system and appear as your confirmed payment in the portal.

"Due in X Days" — What Date Is Shown?

The "Due in X days" badge on your Owner Dashboard shows your next instalment due date — not a building-wide calendar date. The system calculates how many full quarterly instalments your payments have already covered, then shows the due date of the next uncovered period.

  • If you have paid three quarterly instalments, the badge shows your fourth quarter due date, not the building's current quarter.
  • If you paid in advance, the "Due in X days" countdown reflects when your advance payment runs out — which may be months away.
  • Example: UA063 paid $4,590.90 (3 × $1,530.30). "Due in X days" shows 1 December 2026 (Q4), not 1 June 2026 (building's current period).

This ensures the date displayed is always meaningful to you, regardless of the building's payment cycle position.

Understanding Your Balance

  • Total Annual Levy = the full levy amount set for your unit for the financial year (based on your unit entitlement and the approved budget).
  • Total Remaining = Annual levy + opening arrears − payments already received. This is the amount you still owe for the year. Example: Annual levy $7,090.04 − Payment $1,800.00 = Total Remaining $5,290.04. Payments made via DEFT, BPAY, or bank transfer are recorded by the strata manager and reflected in your portal balance.
  • Prior-year credit: If you overpaid last year, the credit is carried forward and deducted from this year's levy. Your "Total Remaining" will be lower than the full annual amount.
  • Prior-year arrears: If you underpaid last year, the outstanding amount is added to this year's opening balance and included in your total owing.
  • Next Estimated Payment = the amount due for your next instalment, adjusted for any credit or overdue amounts carried forward. This is different from "Total Remaining" — it is the next periodic payment amount only.

Water Bills

Quarterly Icon Water (ACT) bills for your unit are available under Finance → Water Bills at /dashboard/finance/water-bills.

  • View bills — See all quarterly bills (Q1–Q4) sorted by due date, with amount, status (Unpaid / Partial / Paid), and balance due.
  • Mark paid — Once you have paid via Icon Water's BPAY or direct debit, you or a strata manager can mark the bill as paid and record a payment reference.
  • Partial payments — If paying in instalments, use the partial-payment feature. Each partial entry is timestamped and appended to the bill notes for a full audit trail. The bill automatically upgrades to Paid when the running total reaches the bill amount.
  • Auto-calculated supply charges — Even when no bill has been uploaded for a quarter, your dashboard shows auto-calculated quarterly supply charges derived from the ACT regulated daily rates: Water Supply $0.6616/day + Sewage Supply $1.6773/day = $2.3389/day × quarter days. This is what you owe to Icon Water for fixed supply costs. You pay supply charges directly; actual water usage costs for the building are an Owners Corporation expense (not charged per unit through the portal).
  • Supply charge estimate tool — The estimates tool (Finance → Water Bills → Estimate) calculates Icon Water supply charges for any date range at the same ACT regulated daily rates. These are identical for all units and do not include usage charges.

Note: Bills are created by the strata manager when Icon Water quarterly invoices arrive. One bill per quarter per unit is enforced — a duplicate submission for the same billing period is rejected with an error. If you believe a bill is missing or incorrect, contact your strata manager.

Trust Accounting & Levy Schedule

The full trust accounting view is available at Finance → Trust Accounting (/dashboard/finance/trust). It provides a consolidated picture of your levy obligations and the building's trust fund position for the current financial year.

  • Levy Schedule tab — Shows your unit's quarterly levy amounts, due dates, payment status (Paid / Partial / Overdue / Pending), and your DEFT BPAY Customer Reference Number (CRN) for each instalment. Use the CRN when paying via BPAY to ensure your payment is matched automatically.
  • Payment History tab — A running ledger of all receipts recorded against your unit across both the Admin Fund and Sinking Fund, with timestamps and reference numbers.
  • Trust Account Balances tab — View the current balances of the building's Admin Fund and Sinking Fund trust accounts, including the last reconciliation date and any variance between the ledger and bank statement.
  • Financial Summary — Shows the building's overall collection rate, total levied vs. total received year-to-date, and outstanding arrears count.

Navigation: From the sidebar, click Finance then select Trust Accounting. The page is available to owners, EC members, strata managers, and admins. Owners see only their own levy schedule and the building-level summary; full transaction detail is restricted to EC members and above.

Levy Fairness — Understanding Your Levy

The Levy Fairness Engine is available at /dashboard/intelligence/levy-fairness (accessible to EC members, chairman, strata manager, and super admin). It shows how your levy compares to the value of building facilities you actually benefit from. Key metrics are the LEI (scheme fairness score, 0–100) and your unit's LBFI (Levy Benefit Fairness Index — how much you pay relative to your benefit share, ideal = 1.0). EC members may use this tool to prepare AGM reports on levy allocation fairness.

New: Explain This Result

Click any row in the unit impact table to open an Explain drawer for that unit. The drawer shows exactly which facilities contribute to the unit's levy — for example, "Pool: $1,200 / Lift: $980 / Insurance share: $2,340" — so owners and EC members can understand why a levy changed rather than just see the final number. EC members can view any unit; owners can only view their own unit.

Model Confidence Badge

A High / Medium / Low badge appears near the LEI score. It reflects data completeness: how many units have real ledger records, how well the asset database covers the total levy budget, and whether specific benefit groups have been defined. A Low badge means some allocations are estimated rather than precisely calculated.
