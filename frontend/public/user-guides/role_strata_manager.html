Strata Manager Resident Handbook

As a professional Strata Manager, you use this platform to streamline building operations, maintain financial accuracy, and support the Executive Committee in their governance duties.

Management Priorities
  • Maintain accurate Financial Records and Budget Projections.
  • Oversee the Approval Workflow for building expenditure.
  • Facilitate AGMs and Committee Meetings.
  • Ensure building-wide Compliance and Insurance are up to date.

Financial Management & Reporting

  • My Property View: Professional building-wide monitoring and management hub.
  • Financial Dashboard: Access real-time data on the Administrative and Capital Works funds.
  • Financial Intelligence: High-level financial analytics and historical building data.
  • Levy Fairness Analysis: 5-tab interactive LBFI analysis at Intelligence → Levy Fairness. Edit facility tiers and cost overrides, add manual cost items, view per-unit benefit vs. current levy comparison, and download a professional AGM-ready PDF report.
  • Capital Funding & Special Levy Workspace: Planned/Pilot workflow at Finance → Capital Funding for major repairs, strata-loan scenarios, staged special levies, owner elections and levy-notice PDF preview batches across all current lot owners.
  • Budget Projections: Develop and review interactive financial forecasts to prepare for future levies and capital works projects.
  • Spending Categories: Fine-tune the allocation of costs to ensure transparent reporting to the Owners Corporation.
  • Unit Plan: Comprehensive access to registered units plan and lot entitlements.
  • Financial Onboarding / Historical Reconstruction: At Financial → Financial Onboarding (or Historical Reconstruction), prepare batches that generate a building's prior-years levy payment history from its proposed budget — useful when a building migrates onto the platform with incomplete financial records. You can create, extract, preview, and submit batches for review. Reviewing, approving, generating, syncing, and reversing a batch require a Strata Admin or Super Admin — see the Historical Reconstruction guide for the full workflow.

Advanced Financial Enhancements

As Strata Manager, you have full access to all advanced financial tools on the platform.

Arrears Recovery Board

Access and manage the Debt Recovery Board at Dashboard → Debt Recovery Board. Units in arrears are classified across four severity tiers:

  • Monitor — Balance owing, no action yet taken.
  • Contacted — Section 83 notice has been sent (within 14 days).
  • DCA Eligible — More than $1,500 owing and 14+ days have elapsed since the notice.
  • DCA Referred — Formally referred to a Debt Collection Agency.

Data source: The Arrears Recovery Board sources data from the full strata roll (unit_levy_ledger — all 87 units), not just owners registered in the portal. Units where the owner has not created a portal account are still displayed; they show "No portal account" and their owner name from the official strata roll. For FY2026, 18 units carry prior-year opening arrears totalling $2,794.97. Current-year levy instalments paid via the portal (Stripe) do not reduce prior-year opening arrears — only officially recorded DEFT/bank imports affect the arrears balance.

Generate Section 83 Notice

Issue a formal demand letter under Section 83 of the Unit Titles Management Act 2011 (ACT) directly from the Debt Recovery Board. The PDF is auto-generated and can be sent to the owner or retained for records.

DCA Referral

Refer a unit to a Debt Collection Agency when arrears exceed $1,500 and at least 14 days have passed since the Section 83 notice was issued. All referrals are logged in the recovery audit trail.

Special Levies

Create one-off special levy charges allocated across all units by their Unit of Entitlement (UOE) at Finance → Special Levies.

Capital Funding & Special Levy Workspace (Planned/Pilot)

Use Finance → Capital Funding to prepare a major-works funding case: capture costs and contingencies, compare special levy and strata-loan options, attach legal and meeting evidence, collect owner votes/elections through AGM or general-meeting motions, and generate a preview batch of levy-notice PDFs for all current lot owners. Preview generation does not issue notices or post ledger entries.

Bank Reconciliation

Record and verify monthly bank statement entries at Finance → Bank Reconciliation. Match payments against the platform's financial records and mark statements as reconciled to maintain an accurate audit trail.

Payment Plans

Create structured instalment arrangements for owners in arrears at Finance → Payment Plans. Set the schedule, instalment amounts, and monitor compliance over time.

Record Expenses & Income

  • Record Expenses: Log building expenses against approved budget categories to maintain accurate fund tracking.
  • Record Income: Log non-levy income such as bank interest, rebates, and grants.

Financial Health Score

The Financial Health Score (0–100) provides a composite view of the building's financial position based on seven components: Surplus Ratio, Arrears Percentage, Cashflow Buffer, Forecast Stability, Budget Discipline, Expense Volatility, and Reserve Adequacy.

True Cost of Ownership

View a per-unit cost breakdown at Finance → True Cost of Ownership, showing admin levies, sinking fund contributions, special levies, council rates, and water charges for each lot. Use this to provide owners with a complete picture of their annual property costs.

Multi-Building Management — Switching Buildings

If you manage more than one building, your sidebar shows a Building Switcher at the top of the navigation panel. Click the building name to open the switcher dropdown and select a different scheme.

  • What switches: All dashboard data, financial figures, resident lists, levy records, and compliance status immediately reflect the selected building.
  • Your primary building: The building you first registered under remains your login home. Switching is session-based — your next login opens your primary building.
  • Access requirement: You must hold an active membership in a building before you can switch to it. Contact your system administrator if a building is missing from your list.
  • Demo building (Acme Strata Demo): Financial figures are seeded demo data; no real levies or resident records are affected.
Technical note: Switching issues a fresh authentication token scoped to the new building. All API calls automatically target the correct data partition. Reload the page if data appears stale after switching.
