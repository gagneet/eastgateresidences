My Finances

This guide explains how levy information is shown to residents in a multi-building setup.

What you see on the page

  • current balance and levy progress
  • Administrative Fund and Sinking Fund contributions
  • payment schedule and due dates
  • historical levy trends for your building

How levy amounts are calculated

The platform stores Administrative Fund and Sinking Fund source amounts ex-GST. Owner-facing payable amounts are then calculated using the active building's Site Settings.

  • gst_registered controls whether GST applies
  • levy_gst_rate controls the percentage used for levy billing
  • per-unit amounts are distributed using your unit entitlement (UOE)

This keeps levy displays consistent across buildings while still allowing each building to use its own finance settings.

Why another building may show different totals

Each building has separate budgets, entitlements, due dates, feature toggles, and branding. Even if two buildings use the same screens, the underlying results can differ because all financial data is scoped by building_id.

Need help?

  1. Check the payment schedule on the page.
  2. Review your building's notices or finance summary.
  3. Contact your building manager or committee contact shown in the app.
