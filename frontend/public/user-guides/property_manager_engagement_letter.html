Compliance Requirements

These are statutory obligations for all ACT rental properties. Non-compliance may result in penalties or inability to rent.

🔔

Smoke Alarms — Mandatory Annual Check

Working smoke alarms are required in all rental properties and must be inspected annually. This service is managed through Smoke Alarms Australia at $109/year and includes unlimited callouts for the duration of the tenancy.

🏊

Swimming Pools — ACT Compliance Deadline: 1 May 2028

All ACT pools must be compliant or exempt by 1 May 2028. Pools constructed after 1 May 2013 should already hold compliance certificates. If unsure of your pool's status, contact the ACT Government.
ACT Pool Safety Standards ↗

🏠

Ceiling Insulation — Minimum R5 Standard

ACT rental properties must meet a minimum of R5 ceiling insulation. If your existing insulation is below R2, an upgrade is required before the property can be re-let. No action is needed if insulation is already R2 or above.

Owner Identification Check

Required once at onboarding for office verification and security compliance. Documents are held securely and used solely for this purpose.
