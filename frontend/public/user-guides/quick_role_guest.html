Guest Information Guide

Welcome to your building. This guide covers everything you need to know for a comfortable and compliant stay. Please read it before or on arrival — your host expects you to follow all rules below.

Your stay in summary
  • You are a registered short-term guest of a unit owner
  • You are bound by the same by-laws and house rules as residents
  • Your host (the unit owner) is responsible for your conduct on the premises
  • Breaches may result in immediate removal and reporting to the Owners Corporation

House Rules

Noise & Quiet Hours

  • 🔇Quiet hours: 10 pm – 8 am daily. No amplified music, parties, or loud gatherings.
  • Keep voices and TV at a considerate level at all times in common areas.
  • No slamming doors, running in corridors, or excessive noise in stairwells.

Common Areas & Corridors

  • Keep all corridors, fire exits, and stairwells completely clear — no bikes, prams, boxes, or personal items.
  • No smoking anywhere inside the building, in corridors, on balconies, or within 4 metres of any entrance.
  • No vaping, hookahs, or open-flame devices anywhere on the premises.
  • Do not prop open fire doors or building entry doors at any time.
  • Report any lift faults or safety hazards to your host or the emergency number below.

Unit Conduct

  • No parties or events that exceed the normal occupancy of the unit.
  • No subletting — you may not let others stay in the unit on your behalf.
  • Treat all appliances, furniture, and fixtures with care. Report damage promptly to your host.
  • No alterations, drilling, or affixing anything to walls without owner permission.
  • Do not remove any furniture or equipment from the unit.

Balconies

  • No hanging laundry, towels, or items visible from outside (including over balcony railings).
  • No throwing or dropping anything from balconies.
  • BBQ use on balconies is not permitted unless your host has confirmed it is approved for the unit.

Pet Policy for Guests

No pets permitted for short-term guests

Short-term guests are not permitted to bring pets (including dogs, cats, birds, or any animals) onto the premises without prior written approval from both the unit owner and the Owners Corporation. Unauthorised pets will result in immediate removal of the animal and may incur a by-law breach notice against your host.

If you believe an exception applies, your host must contact the Strata Manager before your arrival at EC email.

Amenities & Access

  • Gym / Pool / BBQ areas — available to guests accompanied by their host. Hours are posted on-site. Book via the portal if your host has enabled this, or ask your host to book for you.
  • Visitor parking — short-term guest use only (see Parking section below).
  • Lift access — please allow residents priority during peak hours (7–9 am, 5–7 pm).
  • Bike cages & storage — not available to short-term guests unless your host has arranged access.
  • Do not use resident-only facilities (e.g., rooftop access, resident gym) outside bookings or without host accompaniment.

Parking & Deliveries

  • Visitor parking is for temporary guest use only — maximum 24 hours per space per visit.
  • Do not park in numbered resident bays without explicit permission from the bay owner.
  • Overnight parking in visitor bays must be disclosed to your host, who is responsible for notifying management.
  • Street parking near the building may be available — check local signage and time restrictions.
  • Deliveries: Arrange deliveries during your host's available hours. Parcel lockers or the concierge area are available — ask your host for the access code.

Waste & Recycling

  • Use the designated bin rooms on each floor — general waste (red lid), recycling (yellow lid), organics (green lid where available).
  • Do not leave rubbish bags in corridors or at the lift lobby.
  • Bulk / hard waste must be arranged via your host and booked through management — do not place large items in common areas.
  • Cardboard should be broken down flat and placed in the recycling chute or bin room.

Emergency Procedures

IN THE EVENT OF A FIRE — RING 000 IMMEDIATELY TO ENSURE FIRE SERVICES RESPONSE

On hearing the fire alarm

1
Stay calm. Do NOT use the lifts.
2
Check if your door is hot before opening. If hot, stay in your unit, seal the gap under the door with towels, and call 000.
3
If safe to leave, close (do not lock) your door behind you and proceed to the nearest stairwell exit.
4
Follow exit signs to the designated Assembly Area shown on the fire safety notice in your building's lobby St.
5
Do NOT re-enter the building until the Fire Brigade gives the all-clear.
6
If you require mobility assistance, wait in the pressurised stairwell refuge area and alert the Fire Brigade warden.
