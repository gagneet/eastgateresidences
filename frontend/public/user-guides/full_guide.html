Resident-Friendly Comprehensive Handbook

Welcome to the your building Resident Handbook. This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know about living in your building and using our digital platform.

Tip: Use the "Role Handbook" for your specific role for a faster overview of your most common tasks.

1) Getting Started

Registration & Approval

There are two ways to get an account:

  • Self-registration: Visit your building portal and click Register. Provide your email, full name, phone number, and unit/lot number. Your account is created immediately and you can log in once approved.
  • Invitation link: Your strata manager or administrator emails you a personalised link. Click it to open a pre-filled registration form — just set your password and you're ready to go. Invitation links are valid for 14 days.

After registering, ensure you provide your correct Unit/Lot number for verification. You'll receive an email notification once your account is approved.

6) Advanced Financial Enhancements

The following advanced financial tools are available to Chairmen, Strata Managers, EC Members, and Super Admins based on their permission level.

Arrears Recovery Board

The Debt Recovery Board (at Dashboard → Debt Recovery Board) provides a structured view of all units carrying outstanding balances. Units are automatically classified into four severity tiers to guide recovery action:

  • Monitor — Balance owing; no formal action yet. Suitable for recently overdue accounts.
  • Contacted — A Section 83 notice has been issued. The 14-day response window is being tracked.
  • DCA Eligible — Arrears exceed $1,500 and 14+ days have elapsed since the notice. Ready for referral.
  • DCA Referred — Formally referred to a Debt Collection Agency for recovery.

Available to: EC Members (view), Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin (full).

Screenshot placeholder: Arrears Recovery Board – 4-tier severity view

Generate Section 83 Notice

A formal demand letter compliant with Section 83 of the Unit Titles Management Act 2011 (ACT) can be generated as a PDF directly from the Debt Recovery Board. The document is auto-populated with the owner's name, unit details, and outstanding amount. It can be downloaded for postal service or stored against the unit's record.

Available to: Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin.

DCA Referral

When a unit has more than $1,500 in arrears and at least 14 days have passed since the Section 83 notice was sent, an authorised user can formally refer the matter to a Debt Collection Agency. All referral actions are logged in the recovery audit trail for compliance purposes.

Available to: Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin.

Special Levies

One-off special levy charges can be raised and allocated across all units based on their Unit of Entitlement (UOE). This feature is used for extraordinary capital works or emergency repairs that fall outside the approved annual budget. Navigate to Finance → Special Levies to create a charge, set the purpose, and define the due date.

Available to: Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin.

Capital Funding & Special Levy Workspace (Planned/Pilot)

For major repairs or remediation, authorised staff will use Finance → Capital Funding to compare upfront special levies, staged levies, strata loans and legally reviewed mixed funding structures. The owner-facing side links to AGM/SGM/GM voting, personal impact statements, funding elections and hardship requests. The first release generates levy-notice PDF preview batches only; live issue and ledger posting require separate dual approval.

Available to: Strata Manager, Strata Admin, Super Admin; owners receive linked voting/election pages.

Bank Reconciliation

Monthly bank statement entries can be recorded and verified at Finance → Bank Reconciliation. This tool allows the strata manager or administrator to match incoming payments against the platform's financial records, flag discrepancies, and mark each month as reconciled for audit purposes.

Available to: Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin.

Payment Plans

For owners experiencing financial hardship, structured instalment arrangements can be created at Finance → Payment Plans. An authorised user defines the schedule, instalment amounts, and total owed. The system tracks each instalment and flags if a payment is missed.

Available to: Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin.

Record Expenses & Income

  • Record Expenses: Log actual building expenses against approved budget categories to keep the administrative and sinking fund records accurate and auditable.
  • Record Income: Log non-levy income such as bank interest received, government rebates, insurance proceeds, or grants to the Owners Corporation.

Available to: Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin.

Financial Health Score

The Financial Health Score is a composite indicator (0–100) displayed on the Financial Intelligence page. It aggregates seven financial metrics to provide a single, at-a-glance reading of the building's financial well-being:

  • Surplus Ratio, Arrears Percentage, Cashflow Buffer, Forecast Stability, Budget Discipline, Expense Volatility, and Reserve Adequacy.

True Cost of Ownership

The True Cost of Ownership report at Finance → True Cost of Ownership provides a per-unit breakdown of all financial charges, including admin fund levies, sinking fund contributions, special levies, council rates, and water charges. This gives the Executive Committee and strata manager a complete picture of the actual cost to each owner beyond just the quarterly levy.

Available to: EC Members, Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin.

Tax Summary PDF (All Owners)

Every owner can download an annual levy breakdown PDF for their own unit. This document summarises admin fund and sinking fund levy contributions for the financial year and is particularly useful for owners of investment properties who need to provide their accountant with evidence of strata levy payments for tax return purposes.

Navigate to Dashboard → Finance → Levy Status and select Download Tax Summary.

Available to: All owners (own unit only), EC Members, Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin.

Permission Summary: Not all Advanced Financial Enhancement features are available to every role. Owners access only the Tax Summary PDF for their own unit. EC Members can view the Recovery Board, Health Score, True Cost, and their own Tax Summary. Chairman, Strata Manager, and Super Admin have full access to all tools.

Financial Year Data Import

The Financial Year Data Import tool allows privileged users to bulk-load financial data for a new financial year using pre-formatted CSV templates. This replaces manual data entry when onboarding a new financial year, importing actuals from an external accounting system, or migrating from a legacy strata management platform.

Available to: Strata Manager, Super Admin, Chairman.

Navigation

Go to Dashboard → Finance → Financial Year Import (found under the Reports & Analytics group in the Finance sidebar).

5-Step Import Wizard

  1. Select Import Type — Choose one of the four supported data sets: Owner & Unit Details, Annual Levy Summary, Budget Categories, or Per-Unit Levy Status.
  2. Download Template — Click Download CSV Template to get the correctly formatted spreadsheet for the selected import type. Fill in the template using your external accounting or strata management data.
  3. Upload CSV — Drag-and-drop or click to select your completed CSV file. Maximum file size is 10 MB; maximum 500 rows per upload. Only .csv format is accepted.
  4. Preview Results — Before any data is written, the platform validates every row and displays a preview table showing how many records will be imported, how many will be skipped, and a full list of validation errors (if any). Review this carefully. You can abort at this step at no cost.
  5. Confirm & Import — Click Confirm Import to commit the validated rows to the database. A summary card shows the final imported count, skipped count, and any errors encountered during the write phase.

Import Types and CSV Columns

Each import type has its own CSV template. Download the appropriate template from Step 2 of the wizard.

Import Type Template Key Required CSV Columns
Owner & Unit Details unit_owners unit_number, owner_name, owner_email, entitlement, property_type (apartment/townhouse), lot_number
Annual Levy Summary annual_levy_summary financial_year (e.g. 2026-2027), admin_fund_budget, sinking_fund_budget, total_units, total_entitlements
Budget Categories budget_categories category_name, fund_type (administrative/sinking), budgeted_amount, financial_year
Per-Unit Levy Status unit_levy_status unit_number, financial_year, annual_levy, admin_levy, sinking_levy, total_paid, opening_arrears

Constraints & File Requirements

  • File format: CSV only (.csv extension, UTF-8 encoding).
  • Maximum file size: 10 MB.
  • Maximum rows per file: 500 (excluding the header row).
  • The financial_year query parameter (e.g. 2026-2027) must match the data in the file.
  • Rows with missing required columns are skipped and reported in the preview error list.

Error Handling

The preview step (Step 4) runs full validation before any data is written. Each error entry identifies the row number and the specific validation failure (e.g. "Row 14: unit_number is required", "Row 22: entitlement must be a positive number"). You may fix your CSV and re-upload without any side effects.

After a confirmed import, the summary card shows imported (rows successfully written), skipped (rows that did not pass validation), and errors (any unexpected write failures) so you always know exactly what was committed.

Security & Data Isolation

All imported data is scoped to the currently selected building. The API enforces building-level isolation: a user authenticated to your building cannot write data into a different building even if they supply a different building_id in the CSV. Import history is also building-scoped and visible only to users with access to that building.

7) Property Intelligence

The Property Intelligence dashboard at /dashboard/intelligence provides a real-time view of building asset health, predictive maintenance costs, and capital works planning. It is designed for EC Members, the Chairman, and the Strata Manager to proactively manage the building's long-term financial health.

Overview Dashboard

The main intelligence page shows four clickable signal cards:

  • Attention Needed — Assets with recurring repairs or replace-now signals. Click to go to the Asset Intelligence detail page.
  • Upcoming Capital Costs — Next major capital replacement due. Click to switch to the Capital Works tab.
  • Financial Stability — Reserve resilience risk level and balance. Click to go to the Capital Shock Risk page.
  • Capital Works Risk — Next major capital shock window year and risk level. Click to go to the Capital Shock Risk page.

Four KPI cards below show: Asset Health Score (links to Assets page), 12-Month Maintenance Forecast (switches to Maintenance tab), High Risk Assets count (links to Assets page), and 5-Year Capital Need (links to Capital Risk page).

Maintenance Tab

Shows a 12-month predicted maintenance cost chart (all 12 calendar months) using seasonal weighting based on the annual predicted cost. Also shows an Attention Needed panel listing assets with recurring failures.

Capital Works Tab

Shows two sections:

  • 15-Year Sinking Fund Capital Works Plan (2021–2035): Year-by-year contributions vs. expenditure from the approved sinking fund plan. Major capital years (Building Repaint 2029, Intercom & Carpet 2032, Lift Replacement 2035) are highlighted in amber. Includes a bar chart visualisation and a detailed table with opening/closing balances.
  • 10-Year Capital Replacement Schedule: Individual asset replacement items sorted by year, showing CPI-adjusted estimated costs.

Asset Intelligence Page

Navigate to Intelligence → Asset Intelligence for a full asset risk and health snapshot. All table rows are clickable — clicking a row opens a details popup showing:

  • Health score with progress bar
  • Risk score with progress bar
  • Install year, expected life, replacement cost, last service date
  • Maintenance alert details (if the asset has recurring repair anomalies)

Capital Shock Risk Page

Navigate to Intelligence → Capital Shock Risk for a detailed reserve shortfall analysis including Capital Shock Index (CSI), Sinking Fund Adequacy Ratio (SFAR), funding gap, collapse year prediction, and 10-year reserve projection table.

Levy Fairness Page

Navigate to Intelligence → Levy Fairness to see benefit-based levy allocation simulation and fairness scores (LBFI). Compares current levy allocations against benefit-proportional allocations.

For mixed-use capital projects, Levy Fairness outputs can inform apartment, townhouse and shared-infrastructure scenarios, but the approved legal basis and meeting resolution still control any levy allocation method.

Available to: EC Members, Chairman, Strata Manager, Super Admin.
