Staff Invitations Guide

How to invite strata managers, office staff, and committee members to your building. Invitation-based onboarding ensures every staff account is securely scoped to your building from the moment they register.

Overview

Staff invitations allow you to grant access to your building's data to trusted managers and staff members. StrataOS uses an invitation system (not direct signup) to ensure:

  • Security: Only authorized staff can access sensitive financial and governance data
  • Building Scoping: Invitees are automatically assigned to your building's tenant context
  • Role Assignment: You can assign specific roles (Manager, Accountant, Secretary, etc.) with appropriate permissions
  • Audit Trail: All invitations are logged with who invited them and when they accepted

Who can send invitations?

Only users with administrative privileges can invite staff:

  • Super Admins — Can invite anyone to any building
  • Building Admin (formerly Chairman) — Can invite staff to their building only
  • Strata Manager — Can invite office staff to assist with their management responsibilities
Building-Scoped Access: Regular owners and tenants cannot send invitations. If you're an owner and need to invite staff, contact your strata manager.

Step-by-Step: Send an Invitation

1

Navigate to Staff Management

Go to: Dashboard → Admin → Staff & Invitations

2

Click "Invite New Staff"

A form will open where you can enter invitation details.

3

Fill in their details

Provide:

  • Email address — Where they'll receive the invitation link
  • Full name — For identification in the system
  • Phone number — Optional; for notifications
  • Role — Select their primary role (Strata Manager, Accountant, etc.)
4

Assign EC roles (if applicable)

If inviting an EC member, specify their position:

  • Treasurer — Financial oversight
  • Secretary — Governance and minutes
  • General Member — Voting rights only
5

Optional: Add to committee

If this person should be on the Executive Committee, check the "Add to EC" box. They'll be visible in the governance section.

6

Send invitation

Click Send Invitation. An email will be sent to their address with a unique invitation link.

The link is valid for 14 days. After that, you'll need to resend it.

7

Invitee accepts & creates account

They click the link in their email and complete registration. Their account is automatically assigned to your building and given the role you specified.

8

Confirmation

Once they've registered, you'll see their status change to "Active" in your Staff list. They can now log in and access your building's data.

Managing Invitations

View pending invitations

Dashboard → Admin → Staff & Invitations → Pending tab

Shows all invitations that have been sent but not yet accepted.

Resend an invitation

If an invitee didn't receive the email or lost the link:

  1. Find the pending invitation in your Staff list
  2. Click Resend
  3. A new invitation email is sent (the old link is invalidated)

Revoke an invitation

If you need to cancel an invitation before it's accepted:

  1. Find the pending invitation
  2. Click Revoke
  3. The invitation link becomes invalid immediately
  4. You can invite someone else if needed

Modify a staff member's role

After someone has accepted an invitation and become active:

  1. Find their name in your Staff list (Active tab)
  2. Click Edit
  3. Change their role or EC position
  4. Click Save
  5. They'll be notified of the role change on their next login

Deactivate or remove staff

If someone no longer needs access:

  1. Find their name in the Staff list
  2. Click Deactivate
  3. Their account is disabled; they can't log in
  4. All their API tokens are invalidated
  5. (Optional) Click Reactivate to restore access later)
⚠️ Permanent Removal: Deactivation is preferred over deletion. Deletion removes the user completely and is permanent. Deactivation preserves the audit trail.

Multi-Tenant Scoping

Every invitation automatically assigns the invitee to your building.

What this means:

  • When they register via the invitation link, their account is automatically scoped to your building
  • All their activity is automatically filtered to your building — they cannot access other buildings' data
  • If the same person is invited to multiple buildings, they get a separate account for each building and choose which building to work in at login
Example: You invite accountant@example.com to Building A. Later, another administrator invites the same email to Building B. The system creates two separate accounts — one per building. When the accountant logs in, they see a building selector and choose which building to work in.

For Super Admins: You can see a complete audit trail of all invitations sent, accepted, and modified across all buildings.

Email Notifications

Invitation email

When you send an invitation, the recipient receives an email with:

  • Your building's name and details
  • Their assigned role and permissions
  • A unique invitation link (valid for 14 days)
  • Instructions to set up their account

Role change notification

If you change someone's role after they've joined, they're notified on their next login about the change.

Deactivation notice

If you deactivate someone's account, they receive an email notification and can no longer log in.

Troubleshooting

Invitee didn't receive the email

Check:

  • Email address is spelled correctly (no typos)
  • Check their spam/junk folder
  • Ask them if their email system has filters
  • Resend the invitation (generates a new link)

Invitation link expired

Links are valid for 14 days. If an invitee didn't accept within that time, resend the invitation.

Can't modify a staff member's role

You can only modify active staff members. Pending invitations cannot be modified—you must revoke and resend.

Duplicate account issue

If someone has been invited twice (by mistake), contact support. They can help consolidate accounts.

Best Practices

  • Verify email addresses — Double-check before sending to avoid inviting the wrong person
  • Use descriptive names — Include company name if available (e.g., "John Smith - ABC Property Management")
  • Follow the principle of least privilege — Assign the minimum role needed for their job
  • Regular audits — Review your staff list quarterly; deactivate unused accounts
  • Onboarding documentation — Send new staff the User Guides relevant to their role
  • Password security — Remind staff about password best practices

For Super Admins: Global Invitation Management

Super Admins have additional capabilities:

Cross-Building Invitations

Invite a user to multiple buildings by:

  1. Going to Admin → Super Admin Dashboard
  2. Selecting "Invite Staff" → "Multi-Building"
  3. Choosing buildings and roles for each
  4. A single invitation email is sent with options to create separate accounts per building

Audit Log

View all invitations ever sent across the platform:

  • Who invited whom
  • When the invitation was sent and accepted
  • Role changes and deactivations
  • Complete timestamps for compliance
