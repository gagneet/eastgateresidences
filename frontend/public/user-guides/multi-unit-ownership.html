Multi-Unit Ownership

Manage multiple lots in your building complex from a single account

Overview

If you own more than one lot in the Owner Corporation, you can register all of them under one email address and switch between unit contexts without logging out.

This keeps your levy history, maintenance requests, and documents properly separated by lot while giving you a unified dashboard experience.

Registering with multiple units

If you own more than one lot, you can declare them all at registration time.

Step 1 — On the registration form, select Owner as your role and choose your primary unit from the dropdown.
Step 2 — Once your primary unit is selected, a checkbox will appear: "I own additional units in this building complex". Tick it.
Step 3 — Use the additional unit selectors that appear to add each extra lot you own. You can register up to 10 units per account.
Step 4 — Complete the rest of the registration form and submit. Your account will list all declared units pending approval.
Note: You only need to fill in your name, email, and password once — they apply to all your units on the same account.

Already registered? Add a new unit

If you have an existing owner account and later purchase or are gifted an additional lot:

Step 1 — Log in to your existing account.
Step 2 — Go to Profile → My Units (or use the Unit Switcher in the sidebar).
Step 3 — Click Add Unit, select the lot number, and submit. The strata manager will verify your ownership before activating the link.
Tip: If you try to register a new account with an email that already belongs to an owner account, the portal will redirect you to log in and add the unit from your profile — you won't need to create a second account.

Switching between your units

Once you own two or more units, a Unit Switcher dropdown appears in the left sidebar, below the building selector.

  • Your currently active unit is shown with an Active badge.
  • Click the dropdown and select a different lot to switch context.
  • The portal re-issues your session token with the new unit — all subsequent actions (levy payments, maintenance requests, documents) are attributed to the selected lot.
  • You do not need to log out and log back in.
Important: The active unit is a session-level setting. If you open the portal in a new browser tab, it will default to your primary unit. Switch as needed before taking any actions.

Which unit is primary?

The primary unit is the one you selected first at registration. It is the default unit when you log in. You can ask the strata manager to change your primary unit if needed.

Roles and visibility

The Unit Switcher is available to Owner accounts. Committee members and managers with a unit number on their profile can also use it.

Tenants and guests do not have multi-unit access — a separate account is required for each tenancy.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see all my units' levies at once?
Each unit has its own levy ledger. Switch to the relevant unit context to view its balance, history, and payment schedule.
What if my additional unit is not yet in the system?
Only units already registered in the building management system can be linked. Contact the strata manager if your lot is missing.
Can I register the same unit under two different accounts?
No. Each unit can only be linked to one owner account at a time. If you believe a unit is incorrectly assigned, contact the strata manager.
Does my approval status apply to all units?
Your account approval applies to all linked units. Each individual unit link is verified for ownership separately before it becomes active.

Contact your building's Executive Committee or Strata Manager for assistance. Details are available in the portal under Building Information.
