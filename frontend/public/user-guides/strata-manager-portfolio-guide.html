Strata Manager Portfolio Guide

This guide is for Strata Managers who manage one or more buildings. It explains how to use StrataOS to stay across your entire portfolio — from a high-level workload overview down to individual building issues that need your attention today.

Viewing Your Assigned Buildings

After logging in, your Portfolio Dashboard is your home base. It lists every building assigned to you, showing a status summary for each:

  • Green — everything is on track. Levies are being collected, no overdue compliance, maintenance SLAs are being met.
  • Amber — attention needed soon. There may be a compliance item due, a small arrears balance, or a work order approaching its SLA deadline.
  • Red — action required now. Significant arrears, overdue compliance, or an SLA breach has occurred.

Click any building card to go directly into that building's management view.

Tip: Use the Sort by Status option on the portfolio page to bring your red and amber buildings to the top. This is a quick way to triage your morning.

Switching Between Buildings

When you are working inside a specific building, the building name appears in the top navigation bar. To switch to a different building:

  1. Click the building name in the top navigation bar to open the Building Switcher.
  2. Select the building you want to work on from the list.
  3. All screens — financials, maintenance, compliance, documents — update to show data for that building.
Important: Every action you take (recording a payment, approving an invoice, sending a notice) applies only to the currently selected building. Always check the building name in the navigation bar before making changes.

Using the Manager BI Analytics Page

The Manager Analytics page gives you a detailed view of a single building's performance. Access it from Dashboard → Analytics within any building. Key panels include:

  • Levy collection rate — percentage of levies collected this quarter.
  • Arrears summary — total outstanding and which lots are in arrears.
  • Sinking fund projection — is the sinking fund on track for future capital works?
  • Maintenance pipeline — open requests, in-progress, and recently closed work orders.
  • Compliance calendar — upcoming and overdue compliance obligations.
  • Budget vs. actual — admin fund spending against the approved budget, by category.

Identifying Buildings That Need Attention

Your portfolio overview uses the following signals to flag buildings for attention:

  • Arrears threshold — a building flags amber when total arrears exceed a set percentage of the annual levy budget.
  • Compliance overdue — any compliance item past its due date triggers a red flag.
  • SLA breaches — maintenance requests that have exceeded their response or resolution timeframe appear as breaches on the portfolio view.
  • Levy collection below target — if collection rate drops below 85% at any point in the quarter, the building is flagged amber.
Tip: You can export a weekly portfolio summary as a PDF from the Portfolio Dashboard. This is useful for internal reporting to your strata admin or principal.

Workload Overview

The My Workload panel on your portfolio dashboard aggregates tasks across all your buildings in one place:

  • Open maintenance requests awaiting your action or approval.
  • Invoices pending your review.
  • Upcoming AGM or EC meeting preparation tasks.
  • Compliance deadlines due in the next 30 days.

You can click any workload item to go directly to the relevant screen in the correct building — the system switches building context automatically.
