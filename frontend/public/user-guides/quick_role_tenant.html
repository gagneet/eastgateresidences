Tenant Quick Guide

For tenants who need maintenance, bookings, chat, and marketplace (financial/AGM functions are owner-only).

You can do
  • View public documents
  • Submit and track maintenance requests
  • Book amenities (where enabled)
  • Create marketplace listings
  • Use community chat and private messages
You cannot
  • View levy info or building finances
  • Attend/vote in meetings (owner responsibility)
  • Upload documents or post announcements

Top tasks (checklist)

  • Register and wait for approval (may require owner confirmation)
  • Provide photos/details for faster maintenance resolution
  • Follow building rules and booking limits

Key screens (where to click)

  • Documents → Public documents
  • Maintenance → New request / My requests
  • Bookings → Amenities
  • Chat → Community / Private messages
  • Marketplace → Browse / Create listing

Forgotten password

  1. Go to /forgot-password (link on the sign-in page).
  2. Enter your registered email address and click Send Reset Instructions.
  3. Check your inbox (and spam) for an email from your building — click Set New Password.
  4. The link expires in 1 hour. If it has expired, request a new one.
  5. After resetting, sign in at /login with your new password.

Maintenance Requests — New Tenant Portal

Once approved, you can submit and track maintenance requests directly from Dashboard → My Tenancy → Maintenance Requests.

  • Submit with photos and urgency level (Routine / Urgent / Emergency)
  • Track status: Submitted → Acknowledged → Assigned → In Progress → Completed
  • Send messages directly to your owner/agent/manager in each request thread
  • Receive notifications at every status update

After registration — what to expect

  1. Once registered you are redirected to /register/success — your application summary page.
  2. You can update your phone number and address while your account is pending approval.
  3. The unit owner receives a confirmation request by email and a bell notification in their portal (clicking the bell takes them directly to the Tenant & Guest Approvals page). They must approve before the Strata Manager reviews.
  4. Once the owner approves, you will receive an email confirming owner confirmation. The Super Admin and Strata Manager are also notified.
  5. The Strata Manager then has up to 15 minutes to perform a final review. If no action is taken, the system automatically approves your registration.
  6. You will receive a final Account Approved email once full portal access is unlocked.
