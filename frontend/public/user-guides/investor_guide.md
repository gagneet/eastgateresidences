# Investor Guide
## Understanding Your Investor Grade and Lending Pack

### For Owners

---

## What is the Investor Grade?

Your building receives an **Investor Grade** from **A** (excellent) to **D** (high risk).
This grade summarises the building's financial health from an investment perspective.

| Grade | Meaning   | Key Indicators                                                |
|-------|-----------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| **A** | Excellent | Low stress, strong reserves, minimal arrears, high compliance |
| **B** | Good      | Moderate stress, adequate reserves, acceptable arrears        |
| **C** | Caution   | Elevated stress OR low reserves OR significant arrears        |
| **D** | High Risk | Critical stress OR severely under-funded OR high arrears      |

---

## Where to Find It

Go to **Intelligence > Investor Dashboard** or **Owner Dashboard > My Unit > Investment View**.

---

## Understanding Your Grade

### Grade A 🟢

Your building is in excellent financial shape. This is a positive signal for:

- Mortgage applications (lenders view Grade A buildings favourably)
- Rental yield maintenance
- Long-term property value protection

### Grade B 🟡

Good financial health with some areas for improvement. Most well-managed
buildings sit at Grade B. Minor shortfalls in reserves or slightly elevated
arrears typically result in this grade.

### Grade C 🟠

The building has identifiable financial risks. Consider:

- Reviewing whether current levy levels are adequate
- Engaging the EC to address the specific issues flagged
- Checking whether a special levy may be required

### Grade D 🔴

Significant financial risk. This may affect:

- Mortgage applications (lenders may require higher deposits or valuations)
- Building insurance premiums
- Future special levy risk

## Red Flags and Positive Signals

The dashboard shows **Red Flags** (risks) and **Positive Signals** (strengths):

**Examples of Red Flags:**

- Arrears rate above 10%
- Sinking fund below 75% adequacy
- No AGM in the past 18 months
- EC quorum not maintained

**Examples of Positive Signals:**

- Sinking fund fully adequate
- Very low arrears (under 2%)
- High compliance score
- Strong financial health (Healthy category)

---

## Levy Trajectory

The **Levy Trajectory** chart shows how your quarterly levy has changed over the
past 5 years, and projects the next 5 years based on current trends.

The **5-Year CAGR** (Compound Annual Growth Rate) shows how fast levies have been
growing. A CAGR above 5% may warrant discussion at the next AGM.

## Downloading the Lending Pack

Click **Download Lending Pack** to get a PDF report suitable for:

- **Mortgage applications** — contains building financial health summary
- **Refinancing** — demonstrates building stability to your lender
- **Investment analysis** — investor-grade building assessment

The Lending Pack includes:

- Investor grade and rationale
- Building stress score
- Levy trajectory (3-year history + 1-year projection)
- Special levy risk assessment
- True Cost of Ownership summary
- Sinking fund adequacy statement
- Arrears rate

### Privacy Note

The Lending Pack for **your unit** is visible to you only. Other owners' unit data
is not included or accessible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can my mortgage lender access this data directly?**
A: No. The Lending Pack is a downloadable PDF that you share with your lender.

**Q: What does "Special Levy Risk: Low/Medium/High" mean?**
A: This indicates the likelihood that the building will need to levy owners for
an unexpected capital expense in the next 3 years.

- **Low** — Sinking fund is adequate, no major known risks
- **Medium** — Some capital works uncertainty
- **High** — Sinking fund shortfall or major capital works likely

**Q: Who else can see my investor grade?**
A: EC members and Strata Manager can see the building-level investor grade.
Your individual unit's grade and Lending Pack are private to you.
