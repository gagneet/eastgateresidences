Portfolio Dashboard

A cross-building overview for strata managers and administrators.

Who Can See This?

The Portfolio Dashboard is available to:

  • Super Admin — full access, all buildings
  • Strata Manager — buildings they manage
  • EC Chairman — their building only (manager-level access)

Owners and tenants cannot access this page.

Overview

The Portfolio Dashboard shows a summary of every building in your portfolio at a glance. You can monitor financial health, maintenance activity, compliance deadlines, and arrears across all buildings without switching between them.

Navigate to: Dashboard → Portfolio

Summary Cards

At the top of the page, four summary cards provide portfolio-wide totals:

Card What it shows
Buildings Total active buildings in your portfolio
Total Lots Sum of all strata lots across every building
Avg Health Average building health score (0–100) across all buildings
Alerts Number of buildings with low health or critical issues

Buildings Table

Each row represents one building. The columns are:

Column Meaning
Building Building name and ID
Lots Total strata lots in this building
Health Overall building health score 0–100 (A ≥ 85, B ≥ 70, C ≥ 55, D < 55)
Arrears % Percentage of lots with outstanding levy payments
Open WOs Number of open maintenance work orders
Next Compliance Nearest upcoming compliance deadline (e.g. fire safety inspection)
Actions View button to open the building's detail page

Click any row to navigate to the building detail page for a full breakdown.

Health score colour coding:
Green (≥ 75) — Healthy   Amber (≥ 50) — Needs attention   Red (< 50) — At risk

Portfolio Alerts

If any building has a health score below 50, an alert card appears above the table with a summary of affected buildings and the reason (e.g. "Low Health: 40"). Resolve alerts by addressing the underlying issues in that building.

Recompute Button

Health scores and metrics are pre-computed nightly. If you need fresh data immediately:

Step 1: Click Recompute in the top-right of the page.
Step 2: Wait for the spinner to stop — the page will refresh with updated figures.

Recompute calls the POST /community-dashboard/recompute endpoint for the selected building. For a full portfolio refresh, click Recompute on each building or use the admin seed script.

Last Updated Banner

A banner below the header shows when the portfolio data was last refreshed. Metrics are computed daily at 02:00 AEST by the automated workflow. If this timestamp is more than 24 hours old and Recompute has not been triggered, check that the nightly cron is running.

Column Tooltips

Hover over any column header (the ⓘ icon) to see a plain-English explanation of what that metric means and how it is calculated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a building show 0 lots?

The building record may not have the lot_count field set. Run the buildings seed script or contact your system administrator.

Why is the health score missing?

Health scores must be computed before they appear. Click Recompute or wait for the nightly run. If the score is still missing after that, the building_summaries seed script may need to be re-run.

I can see my building but not others — is that correct?

Yes. The portfolio is scoped to buildings your account has access to. Super Admins see all buildings; EC Chairmen see only their own building.
