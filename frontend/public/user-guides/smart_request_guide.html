Smart Request — User Guide

Role: All approved residents (owners, tenants, EC members)

Location: /dashboard/smart-request or from Community Hub → Smart Request

What is Smart Request?
Smart Request is an intelligent triage system. You submit a query or request and the system either answers it instantly (auto-resolve) or routes it to the strata management team for follow-up within 48 hours.

How to Submit a Request

1
Choose a Category
Select the type of request: Maintenance, Levy Query, By-Law Query, Document Request, Noise Complaint, or Other.
2
Fill in the Details
Provide a subject line and a detailed description. The more detail you provide, the faster your request can be resolved.
3
Submit
Click "Submit Request". You'll see a confirmation with your reference number.
4
Track your request
Click "Track Status" to follow your request's progress, or find it in the "My Requests" list on the same page.

What Happens After You Submit?

Stage Meaning SLA
Auto-Resolved Your query was answered instantly from our knowledge base. No further action needed. Instant
Open Your request is in the queue, waiting for initial review by the strata management team. Up to 48 hours
In Review A staff member is actively reviewing your request. Within 48h of Open
In Progress Your request has been assigned and work has started. Varies by type
Awaiting Resident The team needs more information from you. Check your email and the timeline for what's needed. Paused until you respond
Resolved Your request has been actioned. You'll receive a notification. —
Closed The request has been fully closed and archived. —
Overdue The 48-hour SLA has been exceeded. The team will be escalating this shortly. Breach flagged

Notifications

  • You will receive an email notification each time the status of your request changes.
  • A bell notification will also appear in your dashboard notification panel.
  • The email is sent to both your login email and your @ email (if different).

Who Reviews My Request?

All non-auto-resolved requests are reviewed by the strata management team, which includes:

  • Strata Manager
  • Building Chairman
  • Executive Committee (EC) members

Requests are triaged by category and urgency. Maintenance requests may be escalated to a contractor if required.

Reference Numbers

Every submitted request receives a unique reference number in the format REQ-YYYY-NNNN (e.g. REQ-2026-0042). Quote this number when contacting the strata manager about your request.

Tips for Faster Resolution

  • For maintenance: include the exact location (floor, unit number, area), what you've already tried, and urgency level.
  • For levy queries: include your unit number and the financial year in question.
  • For noise complaints: include the approximate time, duration, and the unit number if known.
  • For by-law queries: quote the by-law number if you know it.
