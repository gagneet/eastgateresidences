Requests & Applications — Tenant Guide

Open Dashboard → Requests to search and filter requests available to tenants in your current building. Use My Requests to track submitted items.

Verified Tenant Capabilities

  • Start enabled insurance, pet and access-device request forms when your account has a lot relationship.
  • Use the tenant maintenance or dedicated Maintenance page for repairs, depending on your building setup.
  • Provide required descriptions and attachments only where the form asks for them.

Restrictions

Tenant access is building-scoped and feature-gated. Reimbursement and work-order operations are not tenant workflows. For immediate safety risks, contact emergency services first.
