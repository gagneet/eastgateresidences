Capital Funding & Special Levy Workspace

This planned workspace helps a building handle major repairs, remediation, insurance shocks and unexpected costs without relying on uncontrolled spreadsheets.

Important: The workspace supports administration and decision-making. It does not provide legal, tax, credit or personal financial advice. Hybrid or self-funding structures require recorded legal approval before they can be used.

For Strata Managers and Staff

  • Create a capital funding project and capture quotes, contingency, grants, insurance recoveries and available fund contribution.
  • Compare one-off special levy, staged levy, strata loan and legally reviewed mixed funding options.
  • Review per-lot impact using the current ownership and entitlement snapshot.
  • Check the state or territory rule summary before preparing notices, including notice period, resolution type, allocation basis, hardship disclosure and hybrid legal-review requirements.
  • Attach AGM/SGM/GM motions, legal review, lender documents and approved allocation schedules.
  • Click Generate Levy Notice PDFs to create a preview batch for all current lot owners.

For Owners

  • Review a personalised impact statement showing upfront amount, instalments, estimated interest and total cost.
  • Vote on the relevant AGM, SGM or GM motion when electronic voting is enabled.
  • Submit a funding election or acknowledgement where the approved structure permits it.
  • See whether your lot is affected by a mixed-use allocation scenario, such as apartment, townhouse, garage, lift, facade or shared-infrastructure groups.
  • Request hardship support or ask for payment-plan review without triggering automatic debt escalation.
