Decision Register

Your building's formal record of all owners corporation and executive committee resolutions

What is the Decision Register?

The Decision Register is a permanent, searchable record of every formal resolution passed by your owners corporation or executive committee. Each entry is automatically assigned a unique reference number (e.g. DEC-2026-0012) and records the motion text, voting outcome, resolution type, and supporting documents.

This register helps all residents stay informed about how decisions affecting your building are made, and provides a clear audit trail for governance purposes.

Viewing decisions

Navigate to Dashboard → Decision Register. You will see a list of all resolutions, sorted from most recent to oldest.

Filtering

  • Meeting type — AGM, EGM, EC Meeting, or Special Meeting
  • Result — Passed, Failed, Lapsed, or Deferred
  • Date range — filter by the meeting date
  • Tags — e.g. finance, maintenance, bylaw, compliance

Search

Use the search bar to find decisions by keywords in the motion title, motion text, tags, or decision number (e.g. search "DEC-2026-0005" or "landscaping").

Recording a decision (EC / Strata Manager only)

After a meeting, EC members and the strata manager can formally record resolutions:

1
Click Record Decision in the Decision Register page.
2
Select the meeting type (AGM, EGM, EC Meeting, Special) and enter the meeting date.
3
Enter the motion title (brief summary) and motion text (full wording as read at the meeting).
4
Select the resolution type:
  • Ordinary — simple majority (more than 50% in favour)
  • Special — no more than 25% of lot entitlements against
  • Unopposed — no votes against (abstentions allowed)
  • Unanimous — all votes in favour, none against or abstaining
5
Enter the vote count (for / against / abstain) and select the result (Passed, Failed, Lapsed, or Deferred).
6
Optionally add proposer, seconder, tags, and links to supporting documents (e.g. minutes PDF).
7
Click Submit. The system automatically assigns a decision number (e.g. DEC-2026-0012).

Decision reference numbers

Every decision is assigned a unique number in the format DEC-YYYY-NNNN:

  • YYYY — the year of the meeting (e.g. 2026)
  • NNNN — sequential counter that resets each year (e.g. 0001, 0042)

Use this number when referencing a resolution in correspondence, levy notices, or work orders.

Editing a decision

Decisions can be corrected after the fact (e.g. to fix a typo in the motion text or add a document link). Only EC members and managers can edit. The edit history is logged for audit purposes.

Open the decision and click Edit. Only the fields you change are updated — the decision number and original creation date are preserved.

Frequently asked questions

Can owners vote on proposals here?
No. The Decision Register records outcomes already voted on at formal meetings. For online proposal voting, use Proposals & Voting.
Are decisions from before the portal was launched included?
Historical decisions can be entered manually by the strata manager. Contact your building manager to request backfilling of key resolutions.
Is this register legally required?
Yes. Under strata legislation (ACT UTMA 2011, NSW SSMA 2015), owners corporations are required to maintain a record of resolutions passed at general meetings and EC meetings.
What if a decision is deferred?
Record it with result Deferred and note the reason in the Notes field. Once the motion is brought back and voted on, record a new entry for that meeting.
