Strata Manager Quick Guide

As a professional Strata Manager, you use this platform to streamline building operations, maintain financial accuracy, and support the Executive Committee in their governance duties.

Management Priorities
  • Maintain accurate Financial Records and Budget Projections.
  • Oversee the Approval Workflow for building expenditure.
  • Facilitate AGMs and Committee Meetings.
  • Ensure building-wide Compliance and Insurance are up to date.
Dashboard entry point: Logging in takes you directly to the Management Dashboard (Building Intelligence Hub) with real-time KPIs, fund balances, arrears, and activity feed. Strata Managers are always treated as approved (no approval wait required) and have full sidebar navigation from first login.

Financial Management & Reporting

  • Financial Dashboard: Access real-time data on the Administrative and Capital Works funds.
  • Invoice Management: Create and process invoices, ensuring they are correctly categorized for reporting.
  • Budget Projections: Develop and review financial forecasts to prepare for future levies and capital works projects.
  • Spending Categories: Fine-tune the allocation of costs to ensure transparent reporting to the Owners Corporation.

Governance & Meetings

  • Meeting Facilitation: Upload agendas, minutes, and supporting documents for Committee Meetings and AGMs.
  • Voting Management: Configure motions for online voting and monitor quorum and results in real-time.
  • Compliance & Insurance: Manage the building's insurance portfolio, including renewals and claims. Monitor the compliance checklist for statutory obligations.

Building Operations

  • Maintenance Oversight: Monitor the performance of service providers and the status of building-wide maintenance requests.
  • Service Schedule: Maintain the master schedule for recurring services and inspections.
  • Units & Owners: Ensure the strata roll (Units & Owners) is accurate and up to date, processing transfers as they occur.

User Registration Approvals

As Strata Manager, you are now automatically notified via email and bell notification for all registration events:

  • New Guest/Tenant registered: You receive an FYI email when a guest or tenant registers for a unit with an active owner. The owner has been notified and must approve first.
  • Owner approved (portal): When an owner approves a guest/tenant via the portal, you receive a bell notification + email with a direct link to activate the account in User Management.
  • Owner approved (email token): When an owner approves via email token link, you receive a notification to complete activation.
  • New Owner registered: You receive a bell notification + email to review and approve the new owner registration in User Management.
  • Owner Name Mismatch (⚠): When an owner is approved and their registered name does not closely match the strata roll, an amber warning triangle (⚠) appears next to their name in User Management, and you receive an additional bell notification + email flagging the discrepancy. See Owner Name Verification below.
  • Returning archived user: When a previously archived user attempts to re-register, you receive a notification to restore their account from Expired Accounts.

Owner Name Verification

The Owner Name Verification feature automatically flags owner users whose registered name does not closely match the strata roll (owner_name / owner_name_b fields imported from the Excel unit register).

How it works

  • At registration: When an owner registers, their full_name is checked against the strata roll using a 60 % token-overlap algorithm (e.g. "Anthony McDonald" matches "ANTHONY J MCDONALD"; "Alice Wong" does not match "Avneet Rooprai").
  • At approval: When an admin approves an owner, the check is repeated against the latest unit record. If the names still don't match, is_name_flagged = true and flag_reason = "name_mismatch" are stored on the user.
  • Visual indicator: An amber ⚠ (AlertTriangle) icon appears next to the owner's name in the User Management table. Hovering the icon shows: "Owner name mismatch. Strata roll: <Primary Name> / <Secondary Name>".
  • Notification: The approving admin and all Strata Manager users receive a bell notification and email titled "⚠ Owner Name Mismatch — Unit XX: <Name>".
  • Joint ownership: The flag checks both primary and secondary roll names. A match against either is sufficient to clear the flag.

Recommended actions when a flag is raised

  1. Click Request Info (or Request Profile Update) on the user record to ask the owner to provide identity documents.
  2. Verify documents (e.g. Certificate of Title, rates notice) against the strata roll.
  3. If verified: update full_name in the user record and re-approve — the flag will be cleared automatically on the next approval.
  4. If not verified: archive the account.

Feature toggle

This feature is controlled by the owner_name_verification feature toggle (Feature Toggles → Governance category). When disabled, the ⚠ icon and notifications are suppressed entirely.

Multi-Building Management

If you manage multiple buildings or strata schemes, the platform supports seamless switching between them from a single login.

  • Building Switcher: A dropdown in the left sidebar shows your current building. Click it to switch to another building you have access to. Each switch issues a new scoped JWT token — all data automatically refreshes for the selected building.
  • Network Marketplace Listings: When creating a listing in the marketplace, you can enable Share across network to make it visible to residents in all buildings (not just the current one). Only Strata Managers and Super Admins can create network-scoped listings.
  • Broadcast Announcements: When posting an announcement, tick Broadcast to all buildings to simultaneously create the announcement in every building you manage. A confirmation shows how many buildings received it.
  • Site Settings (Building Config): Each building has its own site settings: EC email, contact phone, handbook URL, and unit plan URL. These power the Contact EC and Resident Handbook links in the sidebar for all residents of that building.
