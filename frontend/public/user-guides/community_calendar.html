Community Calendar

Every building event — levies, meetings, maintenance, bookings, and compliance — in one place.

What Is the Community Calendar?

The Community Calendar is a comprehensive, building-wide view of everything scheduled for your strata community. Instead of juggling separate levy notices, maintenance schedules, and meeting invitations, this single calendar consolidates all event types into a month-by-month grid.

Events are colour-coded by type so you can see at a glance what kind of activity is happening on any given day.

Navigate to: Dashboard → Calendar

Who Can See the Calendar?

Role Access
Owner View all events; see their own bookings highlighted
Tenant View community events and their own bookings; levy due dates shown (read-only)
EC Member / Chairman View all events; can add new events
Strata Manager / Super Admin Full access — view, add, edit, and delete events

Event Types

The calendar displays eight categories of events, each with its own colour indicator.

Event Type Colour Description Source
Community Events Blue Social gatherings, community notices, building-wide announcements with a date attached (e.g. BBQ, working bee, pool closure) Manually added by EC / managers
AGMs & Meetings Purple Annual General Meetings, EGMs, committee meetings, and all formal strata meetings. Includes agenda links when available. Meetings collection (manually added)
Levy Due Dates Amber Quarterly levy payment deadlines for the current financial year. Clicking the event shows the amount due for your unit. Auto-populated from building levy schedule
PPM Maintenance Green Planned Preventive Maintenance visits — lift servicing, fire system checks, pool treatments, garden maintenance, and all other scheduled contractor visits. Auto-populated from PPM schedule
Compliance Deadlines Red Statutory compliance due dates — fire safety statement, asbestos register review, WHS audits, insurance renewal, and any regulatory deadline tracked in the compliance module. Auto-populated from compliance_items schedule
Bookings — Move In / Out Orange Scheduled move-in and move-out bookings for residents using the building's lift and loading bay. Required for coordinating access and avoiding conflicts. Auto-populated from move_bookings collection
Bookings — Amenities Sky Resident bookings for shared amenities — common room, BBQ area, gym, rooftop, or other bookable spaces. Shows the booking unit and time slot. Auto-populated from amenity_bookings collection
Announcements Slate Announcements posted by management that have a specific effective date or expiry — such as water shutoffs, lift outages, or by-law reminders. Auto-populated from announcements with event_date set
Auto-populated events: PPM items, Compliance Deadlines, Levy Due Dates, Move Bookings, and Amenity Bookings are all populated automatically from their respective building schedules. You do not need to manually add these — they appear as soon as the schedule is configured by your manager.

How to Navigate

Month View

The default view is a standard monthly grid. Each day cell shows coloured event dots or short event titles for events occurring that day.

  • Use the < Previous and Next > arrows at the top to move between months.
  • Click Today to jump back to the current month.
  • The current day is highlighted with a bold border.

Clicking a Day

Step 1: Click any day in the grid that shows one or more event indicators.
Step 2: A details panel opens on the right (or below on mobile) listing all events for that day.
Step 3: Click any individual event in the list to expand it and see full details — description, time, location, assigned contractor (for PPM), or booking unit.

Upcoming Events Sidebar

The right-hand sidebar shows the next 10 upcoming events across all categories. This gives a quick linear view of what is coming up without having to navigate month by month. Events in the sidebar are sorted by date ascending.

Filtering by Event Type

Use the filter chips above the calendar grid to show or hide specific event types. For example, deselect "PPM Maintenance" to declutter the view if you only want to see meetings and levy dates.

Adding a New Event (Managers and EC Only)

Strata managers, administrators, EC members, and the chairman can add new Community Events, Meetings, or dated Announcements directly from the calendar.

Step 1: Click the + Add Event button in the top-right of the calendar view. Alternatively, click on a specific day to open the day panel, then click Add to this day.
Step 2: Fill in the event form — select event type, enter title, date/time, location (optional), and description. For meetings, you can attach an agenda document.
Step 3: Click Save Event. The event appears immediately on the calendar and in the upcoming sidebar.
Step 4 (optional): To send a notification to all residents about the new event, tick Notify all residents before saving. This dispatches an email and in-app notification.
Note: PPM Maintenance, Compliance Deadlines, Levy Due Dates, Move Bookings, and Amenity Bookings cannot be manually added from the calendar — they are managed through their respective modules (PPM, Compliance, Finance, and Bookings). Changes to those schedules automatically update the calendar.

PPM and Compliance Auto-Population

Two event types are populated entirely from automated building schedules:

PPM (Planned Preventive Maintenance)

When your strata manager configures the PPM schedule (under Maintenance → PPM Schedule), each scheduled task automatically creates a calendar entry on the relevant date. Common PPM items include:

  • Lift inspection and servicing
  • Fire system check and test
  • Pool water treatment and cleaning
  • Garage door and intercom servicing
  • Common area pest control
  • Gutters and drainage inspection

If a PPM visit is rescheduled by the contractor, the calendar entry updates automatically once the manager updates the PPM record.

Compliance Deadlines

Compliance items tracked in the Compliance module (fire safety statement, asbestos register, WHS audit, etc.) each carry a due date. That due date is mirrored as a red calendar event. Once the compliance item is marked complete, the red event updates to show it is resolved.

Who manages these? Only your strata manager or building administrator can create, modify, or delete PPM schedule items and compliance deadlines. If you notice an error in a scheduled maintenance date, contact your manager.

Bookings on the Calendar

Move-in/out bookings and amenity reservations are visible to all residents so the community can see when shared facilities are in use.

Booking Type What Residents See Privacy
Move In / Out Date, time slot, and unit number of the move booking. Lift and loading bay unavailable during this window. Unit number shown; resident name not shown to other residents
Amenity Booking Facility name, date, and time slot. Example: "Common Room — 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM" Unit number shown; resident name not shown to other residents

To make a new booking, navigate to Dashboard → Bookings rather than adding it from the calendar. Once a booking is confirmed, it appears on the calendar automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't I see any PPM events?

PPM events only appear once the PPM schedule has been configured by your strata manager. If the calendar shows no green events, the PPM module may not yet be set up for your building. Contact your strata manager.

Can I export the calendar to my phone?

A calendar export (iCal / .ics) is planned for a future release. At present, key dates such as levy due dates are also shown on your My Finances page and can be noted manually.

I can see a compliance deadline is overdue — what do I do?

If a compliance deadline is shown as overdue (past the due date and not yet marked complete), this requires urgent attention from your strata manager or EC. Raise it at the next committee meeting or contact your manager directly.

Can I delete an event someone else created?

Only strata managers and super admins can delete events. EC members and the chairman can delete events they personally created. If you notice an incorrect event, contact your manager to have it removed.

The calendar shows a levy due date that does not match my notice — why?

Levy due dates on the calendar are sourced from the building-wide payment schedule, not individual unit notices. If your unit has a custom payment arrangement, the date shown may differ. Contact your strata manager for clarification.
