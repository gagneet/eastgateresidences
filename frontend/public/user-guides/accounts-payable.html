Accounts Payable — Invoice Approval

This guide explains how strata managers review, approve, and schedule supplier invoice payments through the AP approval queue.

Access

The AP approval queue is available to strata managers, chairpersons, and super admins. Navigate to Financial → AP Invoice Approval in the dashboard.

Invoice Lifecycle

Each invoice moves through the following states:

  1. Draft — Created by supplier upload or recurring bill template.
  2. Submitted — Forwarded for validation.
  3. Validated — Duplicate check passed; ready for manager approval.
  4. Approved — Manager has approved; ready to schedule.
  5. Scheduled — Payment queued in the payment system.
  6. Paid — Funds disbursed.
  7. Reconciled — Matched against bank statement.

Invoices can also be Rejected, placed On Hold, marked as Disputed, or enter Pending Resolution at various points.

Reviewing an Invoice

  1. Open the Awaiting Approval tab to see invoices in the validated state.
  2. Click any invoice in the list to open the detail panel on the right.
  3. Review all extracted fields: vendor, ABN, invoice number, dates, and amounts.
  4. Check the OCR confidence percentage — fields below 70% are highlighted and will require manual verification.
  5. Note any ABN warnings or 47% withholding flags.

Approving an Invoice

Click Approve to move the invoice to approved state. If your building is in NSW, a budget cap check (SSMA s.102) runs automatically — invoices that would push a budget line more than 10% over cap are blocked and require a general meeting resolution. QLD buildings apply the BCCM committee spending cap.

Rejecting an Invoice

Click Reject to reject the invoice. A reason can be recorded in the notes field. Rejected invoices are terminal — the supplier must re-submit a corrected invoice.

Scheduling Payment

Once approved, click Schedule Payment to queue the invoice in the payment system. This requires the outbound payment initiation feature to be enabled for your building. Contact your system administrator if the button is unavailable.

ABN and Withholding

Invoices from suppliers with invalid or absent ABNs where the supply exceeds $75 ex-GST will display a 47% ATO withholding warning. You should request a corrected invoice with a valid ABN from the supplier before making payment wherever possible.

Transition History

The detail panel shows a collapsible Transition history section listing every state change, who made it, and when. This provides a complete audit trail for compliance purposes.

Refreshing the Queue

Click the Refresh button in the top-right corner to reload the invoice list. The queue does not update automatically while the page is open.

PDF Documents — Building Address in Footer

Purchase Order (PO) and Invoice PDFs generated by the system include your building's registered strata address in the document footer. The address is read from your building's settings (Admin → Building Settings → General). If the footer shows an incorrect address, ask your strata manager to update the Strata Address field in building settings.

Each building in a multi-building portfolio generates its own PDFs with its own address — documents are never cross-contaminated between buildings.
