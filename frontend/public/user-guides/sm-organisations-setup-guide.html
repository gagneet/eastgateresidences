Setting Up a Strata Management Company

This guide is for Super Admins who are onboarding a new strata management company onto StrataOS. It walks through creating the company profile, adding buildings, setting up management teams, and inviting staff.

Super Admin only: Creating a new strata management company (also called an SM organisation) requires super admin access. Strata admins and managers cannot create their own company — this is to ensure accurate organisational data across the platform.

Step 1: Create the Strata Management Company

  1. Go to Super Admin → Organisations.
  2. Click New Organisation.
  3. Enter the company's legal name, trading name (if different), ABN, and primary contact details.
  4. Select the state or territory the company is licensed in. If the company operates across multiple states, select all that apply.
  5. Click Create Organisation.

The company profile is now created. You can return to it at any time from the Organisations list to update details, add staff, or review performance analytics.

Step 2: Add Buildings to the Company

Buildings (also called schemes) can be added to the company profile in two ways:

  • Manual entry — enter the unit plan number, address, lot count, and type (apartment, townhouse, commercial, mixed-use). Use this for buildings being onboarded fresh onto the platform.
  • Bulk import — if the company is migrating from another system, a CSV import is available from Super Admin → Organisations → Import Buildings. Contact StrataOS support for the import template.
Tip: When adding a building, you can mark it as self-managed or agency-managed. This distinction is explained at the end of this guide.

Step 3: Create Management Entities

A management entity is a team or office within the company. For a small company with one office, you may only need one entity. For larger companies, create one entity per branch or team:

  1. Open the company profile from Super Admin → Organisations.
  2. Go to the Management Entities tab.
  3. Click Add Entity.
  4. Enter the entity name (for example, "Canberra Office") and assign a team lead if applicable.
  5. Use the Buildings tab within the entity to assign buildings to it.

Step 4: Invite Members

Once the company structure is in place, you can invite staff to join:

  • Strata Admin — a senior staff member who manages the company on StrataOS. They can invite additional strata managers, view all buildings, and access agency analytics. Assign this role to the principal or office manager.
  • Strata Manager — a staff member who manages day-to-day operations for one or more buildings. They see only the buildings assigned to them.

To invite a member:

  1. Open the company profile and go to the Members tab.
  2. Click Invite Member.
  3. Enter their email address and select their role (strata admin or strata manager).
  4. Click Send Invitation. They will receive an email with a link to set up their account.
Important: Invited members must complete registration before they can access any buildings. If an invitation expires (after 7 days), you can resend it from the Members tab.

Self-Managed vs. Agency-Managed Schemes

When adding a building, you will be asked to select its management type:

  • Agency-managed — the building is managed by a professional strata management company (the one you are setting up). The appointed strata manager is a staff member of the company. This is the most common arrangement.
  • Self-managed — the owners corporation manages the building themselves without a professional management company. EC members take on the administrative responsibilities. StrataOS still provides all the tools, but there is no strata manager appointment. The strata admin role may be held by an EC member or the building's own administrator.

Both types use the same portal features — the distinction affects who can be appointed as manager and which analytics views are available.

Tip: Self-managed schemes can be converted to agency-managed (and vice versa) at any time. Go to the building's settings and update the management type. All data is preserved during the conversion.
