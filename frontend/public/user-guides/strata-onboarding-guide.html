1. Overview — Platform Structure

The platform uses a two-level hierarchy:

🏢
Strata Management Organisation

A company or owners committee that manages one or more buildings. Stored as core.tenants.

🏗️
Building / Scheme

A single strata plan. Always belongs to one organisation. Stored as core.schemes.

Two types of organisation:
SM-Managed — A strata management firm manages one or more buildings for their clients.
Self-Managed — An owners corporation manages their own building directly. Organisation and building are created together (1:1 relationship).

5. Building Onboarding Wizard (7 Steps)

🧙 Admin → Buildings → Onboard New Building /dashboard/admin/buildings/onboard
Strata Admin Strata Manager EC Admin (self-managed, joins at Step 3) All steps are saved to the server continuously — you can close and resume.
1

Scheme Identity

Scheme name, strata/unit plan number, jurisdiction, building type (residential / commercial / mixed), total number of lots.

Creates: core.schemes + core.onboarding_sessions — endpoint: POST /api/onboarding/scheme/start

2

Location & Plan Details

Street address, suburb, state, postcode, council area, lot entitlements basis (unit entitlement / equal share).

3

Lots / Units Import

Enter lots manually one-by-one, or upload a CSV. For each lot: lot number, unit entitlement, owner name(s), owner email. Secondary owner and current tenant are optional.

Endpoint: POST /api/onboarding/scheme/{"{session_id}"}/lots — idempotent, safe to re-import.

CSV validation is basic — duplicate lot numbers and malformed emails are not caught server-side yet. Double-check your spreadsheet before uploading.
4

Financial Setup

Admin Fund opening balance (cents), Capital Works Fund opening balance (cents), financial year start date, bank account BSB + account number, levy period (quarterly / monthly / annual).

Opening balances become the genesis journal entry in the trust accounting ledger at finalise.

5

EC Members & Roles

Invite committee members by email and assign their role: Chairman (full management + voting), EC Member (committee governance), Strata Manager (operational, if applicable).

Invitations are created at finalise (Step 7), not immediately.

6

Owner Invitations (Optional)

Toggle to send invitation emails to all lot owners entered in Step 3. You can choose to defer this and invite owners later from the Users page.

7

Review & Finalise

Review all entries. System validates: at least 1 lot, at least 1 fund with balance, at least 1 EC member invited. Click Finalise to go live.

POST /api/onboarding/scheme/{"{session_id}"}/finalize — creates all invitations + marks session completed.

7. Inviting Owners & Other User Roles

Owners are always linked to a specific lot. Do not invite an owner without first confirming their lot exists and is correctly entered in Step 3 of the wizard.

Available roles and how to invite them

Role What they can do How to invite Nav path
strata_admin Full platform access for their organisation. Manages all buildings, staff, settings. Created at SM-org creation or via staff invitation /dashboard/admin/users
strata_manager Operational management: maintenance, invoices, levies, announcements. Cannot manage staff. Staff invitation from Admin → Users /dashboard/admin/users
ec_member + CHAIRMAN Committee chair: governance-chair access is represented by role=ec_member with ec_position=CHAIRMAN; can approve work orders, governance votes, and budget sign-off. EC invitation in wizard Step 5, or Users page after go-live /dashboard/admin/users
ec_member Committee member: votes on proposals, approves work orders, views financial reports. EC invitation in wizard Step 5, or Users page after go-live /dashboard/admin/users
owner Views their levy account, financial summary, documents, marketplace, community hub. Bulk via wizard Step 6, or individually from Users → Owners /dashboard/admin/users
tenant Views community hub, marketplace, can submit maintenance requests. Invited by owner from My Unit, or by manager /dashboard/admin/users
reception Building reception / concierge: parcel management, smart requests, view-only access. Staff invitation — role = reception /dashboard/admin/users
real_estate_agent Can request rental certificates, view levy status for their managed lots. Staff invitation — role = real_estate_agent /dashboard/admin/users
service_provider Contractor / supplier: can upload invoices, view assigned work orders. Staff invitation — role = service_provider /dashboard/admin/users
guest Read-only community view. No financial access. Self-registers via public registration page /register

Bulk owner invitation (after go-live)

📧 Admin → Users → Send Invitations /dashboard/admin/users → Bulk Invite tab

Upload a CSV with columns: lot_number, owner_name, owner_email. The system matches against existing lots and dispatches invitation emails. Owners who already have accounts are skipped.
