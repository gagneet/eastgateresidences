Schedule of Lease Covenants (Form 4)

Provisions, covenants and conditions subject to which leases of all units are held. Key provisions include:

  • Lease term expires 7 November 2117
  • Annual rent: five cents per annum if and when demanded
  • Units must be used as single dwellings only (no subsidiary used as habitation)
  • No structural alterations without prior written approval from the Authority
  • No solid fuel heating systems without prior written consent
  • Lessee must maintain the unit in good repair at all times
  • No removal of protected trees without Territory consent
  • All minerals and groundwater rights reserved to the Territory
