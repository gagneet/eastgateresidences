What are essential services?

Essential services are building systems that must be regularly inspected, tested, and certified to meet safety and compliance requirements. They include fire safety equipment, lifts, cooling towers, and other infrastructure where failure could endanger occupants or cause regulatory penalties.

Your building's strata portal provides an Essential Services Log to record every service event — who did the work, when, their licence number, the outcome, and when the next service is due. This creates a complete, auditable compliance history for your building.

How to log a service event

1
Go to Dashboard → Compliance → Essential Services.
2
Click Log Service Event. A form opens.
3
Fill in the event details:
  • Service type — choose from the list above
  • Asset name — a clear label for the equipment serviced (e.g. "Cooling Tower A — Level 2")
  • Service date — the date the work was carried out
  • Technician name — the person who performed the service
  • Company (optional) — the contractor's company name
  • Licence number (optional but recommended) — the technician's relevant licence or accreditation number
  • Status — the compliance outcome (see status table below)
  • Certificate URL (optional) — a link to the compliance certificate if available online, or leave blank to add later
4
Set the next service due date in one of two ways:
  • Enter directly — type a specific date in the "Next due" field
  • Auto-calculate — enter an interval in months (e.g. 6) and the system calculates "next due = service date + 6 months" automatically. Leave the next due date blank when using this option.
5
Add any notes, then click Save. The entry appears in the log immediately.
Attaching a certificate: If you do not have the certificate URL at the time of logging, save the entry first. Once the certificate is issued, open the entry and click Edit to add the URL.

Status meanings

Status Meaning Action required?
Compliant Service completed successfully; equipment is in compliance No — schedule next service per the due date
Advisory Service completed but the technician noted items to monitor or address before next inspection Monitor — record follow-up actions in notes
Pending Certificate Service completed but the compliance certificate has not yet been issued or uploaded Follow up — update the record with the certificate URL once received
Non-Compliant Equipment failed inspection or is in breach of compliance requirements Urgent — remediate the issue and log a follow-up service event

Checking for overdue items

The Overdue tab (or View Overdue button) shows all log entries where the next due date has passed and the status is not yet Compliant. Each overdue item shows the number of days it is past due.

Use the overdue list to:

  • Identify services that need to be booked urgently
  • Prepare for annual fire safety statements or compliance audits
  • Confirm which items have already been serviced (update the log to remove them from overdue)
Non-compliant overdue items are a liability. If a non-compliant service is overdue, notify the strata committee immediately and engage a contractor. Unaddressed essential service failures can lead to regulatory action and may affect your building's insurance.

How auto-calculated next due dates work

When you enter an interval in months and leave the "next due" date blank, the system automatically computes:

next_due = service_date + interval_months

For example: service date of 15 March 2026 with interval 6 months produces next due of 15 September 2026.

The calculation uses calendar months (not days), so 31 January + 1 month = 28 February (or 29 in a leap year), as per standard date arithmetic.

Filtering and searching the log

Use the filter options at the top of the log to narrow results by:

  • Service type — e.g. show only Lift records
  • Status — e.g. show only Non-Compliant records
  • Date range — filter by service date from/to

Results are sorted by service date (most recent first) by default.

Multi-building support

The essential services log is scoped to your building. If you manage multiple buildings, use the building selector at the top of the dashboard to view the log for each building separately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I log the same service type more than once?
Yes. Each service event is a separate log entry. You build up a history of all services for each asset, which is important for demonstrating ongoing compliance.
What if the technician doesn't have a licence number?
The licence number field is optional. However, many essential services legally require a licensed technician — check the relevant requirements for your state and service type before proceeding without one.
Can owners see this log?
Yes. Owners and tenants can view the log in read-only mode. This supports transparency about the building's compliance status.
What is the certificate URL for?
When your contractor issues a compliance certificate (e.g. a fire safety certificate or lift inspection report), it is often a PDF stored on their platform. Paste the direct URL here so it can be retrieved quickly during audits. Alternatively, upload the document to your building's document management area and link from there.
Does the system send reminders when a service is due?
The overdue list shows items past their due date. Proactive reminders before the due date depend on your building's configuration — ask your strata manager whether due-date alerts are set up.
