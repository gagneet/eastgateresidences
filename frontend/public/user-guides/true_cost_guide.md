# True Cost of Ownership Guide
## For Owners

---

## What is True Cost of Ownership?

The **True Cost of Ownership (TCO)** shows you the full annual cost of owning your
strata lot — not just the quarterly levy notice.

Many owners focus only on the levy, but the real annual cost includes:

- Admin Fund levy (day-to-day operations)
- Sinking Fund levy (long-term capital works)
- Council rates
- Water charges
- Maintenance probability cost (expected value of repairs to your lot)
- Capital shock buffer (your share of potential unexpected capital costs)

---

## Where to Find It

Go to **Owner Dashboard > My Unit > True Cost of Ownership**.

You can also view it at **Intelligence > True Cost of Ownership**.

---

## Reading the Dashboard

### The Doughnut Chart

The chart shows how your annual cost is split between components:

| Slice            | Description                                          |
|------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| 🔵 Admin Levy    | Your quarterly levy for admin fund                   |
| 🟢 Sinking Levy  | Your quarterly levy for sinking fund                 |
| 🟡 Council Rates | Annual council rates for your lot                    |
| 🔶 Water         | Estimated annual water charges                       |
| 🔴 Maintenance   | Probability-weighted maintenance cost                |
| ⬛ Capital Buffer | Your share of potential future capital works deficit |

### Holding Period Selector

Use the **1yr / 5yr / 10yr** toggle to see your cost projected over different timeframes.

The 5-year and 10-year projections apply a **3.5% CPI inflation rate** — the same
assumption used in the Capital Works Plan.

### Comparison Badge

The badge shows whether your unit costs more or less than the building average:

- **"+12% above average"** means your unit has higher costs than typical
- **"−5% below average"** means your unit has lower costs than typical

## Assumptions

The TCO calculation uses published data where available. Where data is unavailable,
the following assumptions are used:

| Item                    | Default Assumption                                |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Council rates           | 0.3% of estimated property value if not on record |
| Water charges           | $1,800/year if not on record                      |
| Maintenance probability | 2% of property value if no asset health data      |
| Capital shock buffer    | 0.5% of property value if no shock assessment     |

All assumptions are listed in the "Assumptions" section at the bottom of the TCO report.

## What Affects My Cost?

**Levy amounts** — Set each year at the AGM based on the budget. Higher maintenance
and capital works requirements lead to higher levies.

**Council rates** — Set by your local council. Not controlled by the Owners Corporation.

**Water** — Based on your actual usage and water authority charges.

**Maintenance probability** — Based on the condition of shared building assets.
Well-maintained buildings have lower expected maintenance costs.

**Capital shock buffer** — If the sinking fund is below its 10-year target, your buffer
will be higher. A fully-funded sinking fund has a near-zero buffer requirement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does this include body corporate management fees?**
A: Yes. Management fees are included in the Admin Fund budget and therefore in your levy.

**Q: Does this include my personal contents insurance?**
A: No. The TCO only includes strata (building) costs. Personal contents insurance is separate.

**Q: Is the council rates figure accurate?**
A: If your Strata Manager has entered your council rates in the system, it will be accurate.
Otherwise, it uses an estimate of 0.3% of property value.

**Q: Can I see 20-year projections?**
A: The system currently supports up to 10-year projections. Contact your Strata Manager
if you need a longer-term analysis.
