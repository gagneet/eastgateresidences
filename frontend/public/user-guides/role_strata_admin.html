Strata Admin Handbook

Strata Admins manage organisation-level operations across assigned buildings: portfolio visibility, user and role administration, reporting, settings, workflow oversight, and controlled access to finance, compliance, maintenance, documents, and insurance tools.

Primary responsibility

Keep each assigned building operationally configured, correctly staffed, and safely scoped. Always check the selected building before viewing or changing records.

Access Scope

  • Assigned buildings only: Your building selector controls all building-scoped data.
  • Organisation controls: You may see portfolio, reports, users, and settings where your permission flags allow it.
  • No tenant bypass: You cannot view buildings outside your assigned scope, and you should not use screenshots or exports from one building while working in another.
  • Super Admin boundary: Platform-wide configuration, protected toggles, and emergency overrides remain Super Admin responsibilities unless explicitly delegated.

Menu And Discovery

The sidebar is served by the backend navigation configuration for strata_admin. New advanced menu suggestions rotate through unseen items on login/config refresh. Opening a suggested page marks that feature as seen; dismissing a suggestion starts the configured cooldown.

  • Dashboard: organisation or building overview for the active context.
  • Portfolio: assigned-building list, operational signals, and cross-building review.
  • Users & Roles: user approvals, role updates, and permission flag review.
  • Reports: management and operational reporting.
  • Organisation Settings: settings available to your role and selected building.
  • Advanced tools: finance, compliance, maintenance, workflows, levy management, documents, insurance, GST/BAS, and other enabled modules.

Users And Roles

  • Review registrations: Approve, reject, or request more information for pending users after checking building and unit context.
  • Maintain role accuracy: Use canonical roles such as owner, tenant, ec_member, admin_staff, strata_manager, and strata_admin. Do not create a chairman user role.
  • Handle EC positions: Chairperson, treasurer, secretary, and ordinary committee responsibilities belong on EC membership/position records.
  • Check permission flags: Access to finance, settings, user management, and audit functions depends on explicit can_* flags as well as role.

Portfolio And Building Operations

  • Portfolio dashboard: Scan assigned buildings for arrears, compliance, open requests, and workload indicators.
  • Building context: Switch buildings only through the approved selector or super-admin/strata-admin context path. Confirm the active building before exports or updates.
  • Operational triage: Use maintenance and workflow views to spot overdue work, stuck approvals, and SLA-breached requests.
  • Escalation: Escalate policy decisions to the EC and platform-risk decisions to Super Admins.

Finance Oversight

Strata Admin finance access is oversight-oriented and permission-controlled. The platform should show consolidated labels and calculations consistently across finance screens, but ledger-changing actions still require the correct building context and finance permissions.

  • Finance overview: Review collection, arrears, fund, budget, and levy surfaces for the selected building.
  • Levy management: Check annual levy setup, due dates, and unit-level levy status.
  • Payment matching: Review matching queues where enabled; do not create live financial records from test data.
  • GST/BAS ledger: Use only where the feature flag and finance permission are enabled.
  • Exports: Confirm building, financial year, and audience before exporting reports.
  • Financial Onboarding / Historical Reconstruction: At Financial → Financial Onboarding, add or reconstruct a building's financial history after it has already been onboarded (e.g. a migration with incomplete records). You can prepare, review, approve, generate, and sync a reconstruction batch — reversing a batch and applying a levy-item GST regeneration are Super Admin only. See the Historical Reconstruction guide for the full two-person approval workflow.

Compliance, Documents, And Insurance

  • Compliance calendar: Monitor due dates, overdue items, and evidence requirements.
  • Documents: Keep records in the correct building and access level. Avoid using chairperson-only wording for organisation-admin folders.
  • Insurance: Review policy, claim, and renewal surfaces where enabled.
  • Audit trail: Treat sensitive changes as auditable actions. Record why a change was made when the UI provides notes or evidence fields.

Developer And API Docs

Super Admins and authorised Strata Admins can use the browser technical documentation for integration and operational checks:

Demo safety: The generated Postman environment defaults to building_id=demo. Do not change it to a production building unless the test is intentional and approved.

Daily Checklist

  • Confirm selected building and role context.
  • Review pending users and permission changes.
  • Scan portfolio and building dashboards for overdue operational items.
  • Check compliance, maintenance, finance, and workflow exceptions.
  • Record evidence or notes for material administrative actions.
  • Escalate EC governance decisions to the EC chairperson or committee workflow.
