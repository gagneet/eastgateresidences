# Subsidy Map Guide
## For EC Members and Chairman — AGM Presentation Reference

---

## What is the Subsidy Map?

The **Subsidy Map** reveals cross-subsidies between different unit types in your building.

When all units pay levies based on a flat Unit of Entitlement (UOE) schedule, some
unit types end up paying for facilities they don't use. The Subsidy Map makes this
visible and quantifies the dollar amount.

**Example at East Gate Residences:**
Townhouses pay approximately **$42,000/year** towards apartment-specific facilities
(lifts, high-rise fire systems, corridor cleaning) through the flat UOE levy structure,
despite not having access to or benefit from these facilities.

---

## Where to Find It

Go to **Intelligence > Levy Fairness > Subsidy Map** (tab 6).

---

## Reading the Heatmap

The heatmap shows a grid:

- **Rows** = Unit types (Apartments, Townhouses)
- **Columns** = Facility categories (Lift, Pool, Fire System, Corridor Cleaning, etc.)
- **Cell colour** = Subsidy direction

| Colour   | Meaning                                                              |
|----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 🔴 Red   | This unit type is paying **more** than their fair share              |
| 🟢 Green | This unit type is being subsidised (paying **less** than fair share) |
| ⬜ White  | Fair allocation — payment matches benefit                            |

---

## Understanding the Numbers

Each cell shows:

- **Annual Cost** of the facility
- **UOE Share** — what this unit type pays via flat levy
- **Benefit Share** — what this unit type should pay based on who uses the facility
- **Subsidy Amount** — the difference (positive = overpaying, negative = underpaying)

---

## Key Concepts

### Benefit Group

Every facility is assigned to a **benefit group** based on who benefits from it:

- **APARTMENTS_ONLY** — only apartments use this facility
- **TOWNHOUSES_ONLY** — only townhouses use this facility
- **ALL_LOTS** — all units benefit

Facilities in APARTMENTS_ONLY still get spread across all units via UOE, creating a cross-subsidy.

### LBFI Score

The **Levy Benefit Fairness Index** measures how equitably costs are allocated.

- Score of 1.0 = perfect equity
- Score < 1.0 = some cross-subsidy exists

## Using This at the AGM

The Subsidy Map is designed to support transparent AGM discussions about levy fairness.

### Suggested Agenda Item

*"Facility Cost Allocation Review"*

Present the heatmap and:

1. Show the total cross-subsidy amount
2. Identify the top 3 facilities driving the cross-subsidy
3. Discuss whether the current UOE schedule reflects actual benefit

### Possible Outcomes

- **No change** — Owners accept current allocation as a shared community cost
- **Adjusted benefit groups** — Update which facilities are APARTMENTS_ONLY vs ALL_LOTS
- **Differential levies** — A specialist strata accountant can advise on compliance with strata law

## Recalculating the Map

The Subsidy Map is computed from your current building data.

Click **Recalculate** (EC/Admin only) to refresh it after:

- Adding new building assets
- Updating benefit group assignments
- Importing new financial year data

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How accurate is the ~$42,000 cross-subsidy figure?**
A: It is calculated from actual building asset data and actual levy amounts.
The figure is as accurate as the data entered into the system. If assets
or costs are missing, the figure may be understated.

**Q: Does this mean townhouses are being treated unfairly?**
A: Not necessarily. The UOE levy schedule is set by the strata plan and
agreed by owners. The Subsidy Map makes it transparent, but whether to
change the allocation is a decision for the owners corporation.

**Q: Can I see the subsidy for my specific unit?**
A: Yes. Click on any unit type in the heatmap to see the per-unit breakdown,
or go to **My Unit > Subsidy Position**.
