Registration Guide

How to create a new account on the StrataOS platform — via self-registration or an invitation link.

Overview

StrataOS is a multi-building platform where each strata building is a completely isolated environment. When you register, the system:

  1. Creates a secure account linked to your building
  2. Assigns you a role (Owner, Tenant, or Staff) with appropriate access
  3. Scopes your session so you only see your building's data

Users from different buildings cannot see each other's data.

Who can register?

Registration is typically available to:

  • Building owners — self-registration after unit purchase
  • Tenants — self-registration if the building allows it
  • Strata managers — invited by super admin
  • Staff — invited by strata manager
Note: Clean-slate installations begin with only 2 super admin accounts. All other users are invited by administrators.

Registration Flow

User clicks "Register" (or opens an invitation link) ↓ Enter email, password, full name, phone ↓ System validates email and unit number ↓ Account is created and linked to your building ↓ You're automatically logged in ↓ Your session is scoped to your building only

Key points:

  • Email — Must be unique within your building
  • Password security — Passwords are securely hashed; never stored in plain text
  • Building assignment — Your account is automatically linked to your building
  • Session scoping — All your activity is scoped to your building only
  • Data isolation — Other buildings' data is never accessible from your account

Step-by-Step: Self Registration

1

Navigate to registration page

Go to the Register link on the login page or the building's public portal.

2

Select your role (if applicable)

Choose whether you're registering as an Owner or Tenant. The form may be pre-filled if the building restricts registration.

3

Enter your details

Provide:

  • Email address — Must be unique (case-insensitive)
  • Full name — As it appears on title deed or lease
  • Phone number — For notifications and recovery
  • Unit number — Your apartment or townhouse number
4

Create a password

Choose a strong password (at least 12 characters, mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols). You can also use a passphrase.

⚠️ Store your password securely. We cannot retrieve it if lost.

5

Accept terms and register

Review the privacy policy and terms of service, then click Create Account.

6

You're registered!

Your account has been created. You're automatically logged in and can access your building's dashboard.

✓ Success: You now have an account scoped to your building. All your data access is automatically filtered to your building only.

Registration via Invitation Link

Your strata manager or administrator may send you a personalised invitation link instead of asking you to self-register. This is the preferred method for staff, EC members, and managed buildings.

How it works: The invitation email contains a secure link that is unique to you. When you click it, the registration form opens with your email address and role already pre-filled.
1

Receive the invitation email

You'll receive an email from the platform with a subject like "You've been invited to [Building Name]". Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

2

Click the invitation link

The link is valid for 14 days. After that, ask your administrator to resend the invitation.

3

Complete your profile

Your email address and assigned role are pre-filled. You only need to enter your full name, phone number, and choose a password.

4

Your account is ready

Once you submit, your account is created and you're logged in automatically. Your access level matches the role your administrator assigned.

⚠️ Invitation links are single-use. Each link can only be used once to create one account. If you accidentally close the page, contact your administrator to resend.

Building-Scoped Access: Why It Matters

Each building on the platform is completely isolated. When you log in, your session is automatically scoped to your building — you can only see your own building's residents, finances, documents, and communications.

This means:

  1. You cannot accidentally view or edit another building's data
  2. Residents, owners, and staff from other buildings cannot see your building's information
  3. If you belong to multiple buildings (e.g. a property portfolio owner or multi-site strata manager), you will see a building selector on login to choose which building to work in
Building Selector: Super Admins and multi-building users see a building switcher in the top navigation bar. Switching buildings changes all data displayed — no information crosses between buildings.

Troubleshooting

Email already exists

If you see "Email already registered," that email is already in use within your building. Try:

  • Checking if you already have an account
  • Using a different email address
  • Contacting your strata manager if you believe this is an error

Password rejected

Passwords must be:

  • At least 12 characters long
  • Include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols
  • Not a common phrase or pattern

Can't see my building in the list

Your building may have a registration restriction. Contact your strata manager or administrator to:

  • Enable public registration
  • Send you an invitation link
  • Manually create your account

Session or login error after registration

If you see an error after registering or logging in:

  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Log out and log back in
  • Try a different browser or device
  • Contact support if the issue persists

For Administrators: Registration Settings

Super Admins and Strata Managers can control registration for their building:

Registration Feature Toggle

Setting: Dashboard → Building Settings → Registration

  • Public Registration Enabled — Anyone can self-register as Owner or Tenant
  • Managers Only — Only invited staff can register
  • Closed — No self-registration; super admin must manually invite users

Building Context

When you invite a user, the system automatically assigns them to your building. Their account is scoped to your building from the moment they accept the invitation — they only ever see your building's data.

If they need access to another building, a separate invitation must be sent by that building's administrator.

Security Best Practices

  • Use a unique password — Don't reuse passwords from other services
  • Enable 2FA if available — Add an extra layer of security
  • Keep your email private — Don't share it publicly or in forums
  • Log out on shared computers — Always log out when using public or shared devices
  • Report suspicious activity — Contact your strata manager immediately if you notice unauthorized access
⚠️ Keep your login details private: Never share your password or account credentials. If you believe your account has been accessed without your permission, contact your strata manager immediately to have your account secured.
