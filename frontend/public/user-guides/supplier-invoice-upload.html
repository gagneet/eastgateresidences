Submitting a Supplier Invoice

This guide explains how to submit an invoice to your building's strata management team using the supplier invoice upload portal.

Before You Begin

  • Your invoice must be in PDF format (maximum 20 MB).
  • Ensure your Australian Business Number (ABN) appears on the invoice.
  • You must have been granted supplier access by the strata manager.

Step-by-Step: Uploading Your Invoice

1. Navigate to the upload page

Go to Supplier → Submit Invoice in the navigation menu, or visit the direct link provided by your strata manager.

2. Select your invoice file

Drag and drop your PDF invoice onto the upload area, or click it to open a file picker. The selected filename will be shown once a file is chosen.

3. Submit the invoice

Click Submit Invoice. The system will automatically extract key details — vendor name, ABN, invoice number, dates, and amounts — using optical character recognition (OCR).

4. Review the extraction results

After a successful upload, a summary card shows all extracted fields. Check that the details look correct. If any fields show low confidence, the strata manager will review them manually.

ABN Verification

Your ABN is verified automatically against the ATO ABR register. A green shield means your ABN was verified successfully. An amber warning means the ABN could not be verified — contact your strata manager if this appears unexpectedly.

47% ATO Withholding

If your ABN is invalid or absent and the supply value exceeds $75 (ex-GST), Australian tax law requires the payer to withhold 47% of the payment. To avoid withholding, ensure your valid ABN is printed on every invoice.

Duplicate Invoice Detection

If the portal detects that you have already submitted the same invoice, you will see a Duplicate invoice detected error. Do not re-submit the same invoice — contact your strata manager to check the status of the original submission.

What Happens Next?

  1. Your invoice is saved as a draft and enters the strata manager's approval queue.
  2. The manager reviews, approves (or rejects), and schedules payment.
  3. You will be notified of the outcome by email.

Need Help?

Contact your building's strata manager directly if you have questions about the status of your invoice or need to provide additional information.
