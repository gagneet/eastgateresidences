EC Member Quick Guide

For EC members managing documents, announcements, maintenance workflows, finance tools, and meetings/AGM setup.

You can do
  • Upload documents and maintain document records
  • Post announcements and notifications (where enabled)
  • Review and manage maintenance requests
  • Access Financial Intelligence, Budget Projections, Collection Rate dashboard
  • Create meetings and publish minutes (where enabled)
  • View My Property View management dashboard
  • Access Resident Management — view and manage user accounts
  • View Owner Details — unit ownership and levy information
  • Review Administrative Requests, Ownership Transfers, Lease Renewals
  • Manage Account Expiry and view Activity Logs
  • Access Asset Register and Strata Roll
  • Approve / reject Tenant & Guest registrations
  • Upload Financial Data
You cannot
  • Change system configuration (feature toggles, email settings, access control) — Super Admin only
  • Create or modify staff user accounts — Super Admin only
  • Set Chairman-only document access levels — Chairman only
  • Approve invoices — Chairman/Strata Manager only

Top tasks (checklist)

  • Triaging maintenance requests daily by priority
  • Assigning contractors and tracking progress
  • Publishing meeting minutes/notices with correct access
  • Posting time-sensitive announcements
  • Maintaining accurate finance entries and reports

My Property View — Personal Finance Dashboard

EC Members who own a unit can access their personal Owner Dashboard via Overview → My Property View in the sidebar. This is the /dashboard/regular page.

  • "Next Estimated Payment" — shows the amount due for your next instalment only, not the total remaining for the year. Advance payments are automatically reflected: if you have pre-paid, the displayed amount shows only what remains for the upcoming period.
  • Advance payments — If you pay more than one instalment at once, the dashboard correctly calculates your next obligation using the Session-53 algorithm:
    net_credit = total_paid − opening_arrears
    periods_funded = floor(net_credit / period_levy)
    next_payment = (periods_funded + 1) × period_levy − net_credit
  • "Due in X days" badge — shows your next instalment due date, not the building's current quarter date. If you have paid three quarters in advance, the badge shows your fourth quarter due date. Your building's levy due dates (check financial settings): March 1, June 1, September 1, December 1.
  • "Back to Management View" button — returns you to the building-wide Management Dashboard.
Example: If your quarterly levy is $1,530.30 and you paid $4,590.90 (3 quarters) in advance, "Next Estimated Payment" shows $1,530.30 for Q4 — not the $6,121.00 full-year balance. "Due in X days" shows 1 December 2026 (your Q4 date), not 1 June 2026 (the building's current period).

Levy Fairness Engine

The Levy Fairness Engine (/dashboard/intelligence/levy-fairness) provides a facility-level cost allocation model that shows how much each lot pays versus the value of shared facilities it actually benefits from. It computes three key metrics:

  • LEI (Levy Equity Index): A 0–100 scheme-wide fairness score. Higher is fairer. 100 = no cross-subsidies.
  • SEI (Subsidy Exposure Index): The fraction of a unit's levy that is a cross-subsidy (or over-payment relative to its benefit share).
  • LBFI (Levy Benefit Fairness Index): Per-unit ratio of levy paid to benefit received — ideal value is 1.0.

EC members can run a recompute with optional transition caps (e.g., max 15% increase per unit per year) to model phased implementation. The Monte Carlo module also forecasts the probability of a special levy being required over the next 10 years. Download options: PDF AGM report, PPTX presentation, CSV unit impact data.

Explain This Result (new)

Click any row in the unit table to open the Explain drawer for that unit. It shows the facility-by-facility breakdown of what drives the proposed levy — useful when presenting AGM results or responding to owner queries. EC members can view any unit.

Model Confidence Badge (new)

A High / Medium / Low confidence badge is displayed near the LEI score. It is calculated from three factors: ledger data coverage (% units with real levy records), asset database completeness, and benefit group granularity. Review the factors list when the badge is Medium or Low before using the model for formal AGM decisions.

Distribution Tab (new)

The Distribution tab shows a before/after histogram of levy amounts across all units. The equity improvement figure shows how much the standard deviation of levies decreases under the proposed model — a positive number means the proposed allocation is more evenly spread.

Cross-Subsidy Report (new)

The Cross-Subsidy tab summarises which unit types (Apartments, Townhouses, Penthouses) are net contributors or recipients across all facilities. Download the full report as CSV via the export button. This is valuable evidence for AGM motions on levy model changes.

Permission Slugs (RBAC System)

As of Session 63, the platform uses a granular RBAC + ABAC permission system. Your EC Member role grants the following permission slugs (checked server-side on every request):

Permission slug What it allows
financial.view View all building financials and reports
financial.export Export financial data (CSV, PDF)
committee.vote Vote on committee motions at AGM / meetings
workorder.create / workorder.update Raise and update maintenance work orders
workorder.review Review contractor reports and progress
documents.upload / documents.view Upload documents and view all building documents
building.view View building profile and configuration
user.view View resident and owner records

All permissions are scoped to your assigned building(s). Chairman role grants additional slugs including financial.approve_invoice and committee.finalize_minutes.
